# Notes: Periodic motion (পর্যায়বৃত্ত গতি)

Book: physics-9-10 › 2.2 › p37 (order 1: the first of two figures on the page; the second is simple harmonic motion)

## What the page shows
The first paragraph of page 37, পর্যায়বৃত্ত গতি (Periodic Motion), has no picture of its own (চিত্র ২.০২, the girl
on a swing, belongs to সরল স্পন্দন গতি below it). The text: a motion that repeats after a fixed time is periodic;
that time is its period (পর্যায়কাল); a particle in periodic motion passes every point of its path once a period at
the same velocity (একই বেগে); the heartbeat is periodic; periodic motion can be on a circular path (a fan's
blade), an elliptical one (Halley's comet round the Sun), a straight line (a mass bouncing on a spring) or any
other shape. Screenshot: `reference/page-37.png`.

## The one idea to teach
A motion that repeats itself after a fixed time is পর্যায়বৃত্ত গতি; that fixed time is its পর্যায়কাল, whatever the
shape of the path.

## Terms (as in the book)
পর্যায়বৃত্ত গতি, পুনরাবৃত্তি, পর্যায়কাল, বস্তুকণা, গতিপথ, বেগ, হৃৎপিণ্ডের স্পন্দন, বৃত্তাকার, উপবৃত্তাকার,
সরলরৈখিক, ফ্যানের পাখা, হ্যালির ধূমকেতু, কক্ষপথ, স্প্রিং.

## How it works
- Four examples (chips in the panel: ফ্যান, ধূমকেতু, স্প্রিং, হৃৎপিণ্ড), each with the shape of its path named on
  the 3D view (বৃত্তাকার পথ, উপবৃত্তাকার পথ, সরলরৈখিক পথ, হৃৎপিণ্ডের স্পন্দন):
  - a ceiling fan seen from below, a coloured sticker under one blade tip (the particle); 300 turns a minute
    (0.2 s), shown 10 times slower;
  - Halley's comet round the Sun: Kepler motion (fast near the Sun, slow far away), the tail pointing away from
    the Sun and long only near it; 76 years a lap, shown at 10 years a second; the orbit is drawn with e = 0.86
    instead of 0.967 (the caption says "মাপমতো নয়");
  - a 200 g mass on a 5.5 N/m spring on a lab stand, a pointer on the mass (1.2 s, real time);
  - an anatomical heart beating at 75 a minute (0.8 s, real time) with an ECG strip running past a pen.
- A mark (দাগ, orange) on the path. The first time the particle passes it the clock (top left) starts; each later
  pass is one repetition: a tick, the mark flashes, its label counts (পুনরাবৃত্তি ১, ২…), and the panel shows
  পর্যায়কাল = মোট সময় ÷ পুনরাবৃত্তি with live numbers and every repetition's time ("০.২০, ০.২০, ০.২০: সব সমান").
- The student drags the mark anywhere on the path (or taps the path; arrow keys move it): the count starts again
  and the period stays the same. A green arrow at the mark (বেগ) shows the velocity there; it is the same every
  time round (the book's "প্রতিটি বিন্দুকে এক পর্যায়কাল পরপর একই বেগে"), and for the comet it is long near the
  Sun and short far away. On the spring a middle point is passed twice a period (down and up); only the passes in
  the same direction (going down) count, so the period comes out right.
- থামাও / চালাও pauses; keys: space or p, ←/→ (mark), 1–4 (examples), f/v (views).
- Test (5 questions, tap answers, wrong answers from real mistakes): the period from a count (÷ the other way,
  ÷ (n − 1), not dividing); the shape of the path shown (its name hidden); the velocity one period later at the
  mark; a fan slowing down after it is switched off (each turn longer: not periodic, even on a circle); the
  heartbeat (60 ÷ beats a minute; mistakes: the other way, forgetting minutes → seconds) or which motion is
  periodic.
- Simple harmonic motion (equilibrium point, speed zero at the ends) is the next figure on this page and is not
  taught here.

## Open questions
- Halley's real period varies between about 74 and 79 years; the text says "প্রায় ৭৬ বছর" and the model uses 76.
- The ECG trace is stylised (P, QRS, T waves at 75 beats a minute on 0.2 s squares).
- Chip label "ফ্যান" (the book says "ফ্যানের পাখা", used in the text and the test); the long label did not fit
  four chips on a phone held sideways.
