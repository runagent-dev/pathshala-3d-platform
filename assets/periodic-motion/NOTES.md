# Notes: Periodic motion (পর্যায়বৃত্ত গতি)

Book: physics-9-10 › 2.2 › p37 (order 1: the first of two figures on the page; the second is simple harmonic motion)

## What the page shows
The first paragraph of page 37, পর্যায়বৃত্ত গতি (Periodic Motion), has no picture of its own (চিত্র ২.০২, the girl
on a swing, belongs to সরল স্পন্দন গতি below it). The text: a motion that repeats after a fixed time is periodic;
that time is its period (পর্যায়কাল); a particle in periodic motion passes every point of its path once a period at
the same velocity (একই বেগে); the heartbeat is periodic; periodic motion can be on a circular path (a fan's
blade), an elliptical one (Halley's comet round the Sun), a straight line (a mass bouncing on a spring) or any
other shape. Screenshot: `reference/page-37.png`.

## The one idea to teach
A motion that repeats itself after a fixed time is পর্যায়বৃত্ত গতি; that fixed time is its পর্যায়কাল, whatever the
shape of the path.

## Terms (as in the book)
পর্যায়বৃত্ত গতি, পুনরাবৃত্তি, পর্যায়কাল, বস্তুকণা, গতিপথ, বেগ, হৃৎপিণ্ডের স্পন্দন, বৃত্তাকার, উপবৃত্তাকার,
সরলরৈখিক, ফ্যানের পাখা, হ্যালির ধূমকেতু, কক্ষপথ, স্প্রিং.

## How it works
- Four examples (chips in the panel: ফ্যান, ধূমকেতু, স্প্রিং, হৃৎপিণ্ড), each with the shape of its path named on
  the 3D view (বৃত্তাকার পথ, উপবৃত্তাকার পথ, সরলরৈখিক পথ, হৃৎপিণ্ডের স্পন্দন) and a one-line note on time and scale
  at the bottom left:
  - a ceiling fan seen from below (painted metal blades), a coloured sticker under one blade tip (the particle);
    300 turns a minute (0.2 s), shown 10 times slower;
  - Halley's comet round the Sun: Kepler motion (fast near the Sun, slow far away), an icy head with a bright
    cloud, and a narrow tail that points away from the Sun, bends back from the motion and is long only near the
    Sun; 76 years a lap, shown at 10 years a second; the orbit is drawn with e = 0.86 instead of 0.967 ("মাপমতো
    নয়"); the mark starts near the Sun, where the comet is fastest;
  - a 500 g mass on a 30.8 N/m spring on a lab stand (T = 2π√(m/k) = 0.8 s, real time): the spring (natural length
    8 cm) hangs gT²/4π² ≈ 16 cm longer and swings 7.5 cm each way; the particle is a coloured dot on the mass;
  - an anatomical heart beating 75 times a minute (0.8 s, real time) that gives a visible squeeze after each R wave,
    with an ECG strip running past a pen (under the heart on tall views, beside it on wide ones). The paper moves at
    a fixed speed (0.2 s a big square), so in the test a faster heart puts the beats closer together.
- A mark (দাগ, orange) on the path. The first time the particle passes it the clock (ঘড়ি, top left) starts; each
  later pass is one repetition: a tick, the mark flashes, its label counts (পুনরাবৃত্তি ১, ২…), মোট সময় takes the
  clock's reading (it lights up), and the panel shows পর্যায়কাল = মোট সময় ÷ পুনরাবৃত্তি. Under it a time strip
  draws every repetition as a bar as long as the time it took, the running one growing at the right end: all bars
  equal, the period. The line under it says it in the book's words.
- The particle carries its velocity arrow (green): on the comet long near the Sun and short far away, on the spring
  shrinking and turning at the ends. At the first pass a faint copy stays at the mark (দাগে বেগ); one period later
  the particle's arrow lands exactly on it (the book's "প্রতিটি বিন্দুকে এক পর্যায়কাল পরপর একই বেগে").
- The student drags the mark anywhere on the path (or taps the path; arrow keys move it): the count starts again
  and the period stays the same. On the spring a middle point is passed twice a period (down and up); only the
  passes going down count, so the period comes out right.
- থামাও / চালাও pauses; keys: space or p, ←/→ (mark), 1–4 (examples), f/v (views).
- Test (5 questions, tap answers, always seen from the front, wrong answers from real mistakes): the period from
  a count (÷ the other way, ÷ (n − 1), not dividing); the shape of the path shown (its name hidden); the velocity at
  the mark one period later compared with now; a fan slowing down after it is switched off (its turns drawn as
  growing bars on the view: not periodic, even on a circle); the heartbeat (60 ÷ beats a minute; mistakes: the
  other way, forgetting minutes → seconds, the number of beats) or which motion is periodic. The worked answer
  starts with the answer.
- Simple harmonic motion (equilibrium point, speed zero at the ends) is the next figure on this page and is not
  taught here (the spring's arrow does shrink at the ends, as it really does).

## Open questions
- Halley's real period varies between about 74 and 79 years; the text says "প্রায় ৭৬ বছর" and the model uses 76.
- The ECG trace is stylised (P, QRS, T waves); the paper runs at twice the usual 25 mm/s for a clearer trace.
- Chip label "ফ্যান" (the book says "ফ্যানের পাখা", used in the text and the test); the long label did not fit
  four chips on a phone held sideways.
