# Notes: Change of State (পদার্থের অবস্থার পরিবর্তনে তাপের প্রভাব)

Book: physics-9-10 › 6.4 › p175–178 (the page images are in `reference/`)

## What the page shows
Topic 6.4 has **one picture, figure ৬.০৮ (p176)**: a graph of temperature (°C, −20 to 120, gridlines every 20, labels
0 to 100) against time (s, 8 squares, no numbers). A solid heated at a steady rate: the line climbs from −20 °C to 0 °C,
is **flat at 0 °C** (squares 1 to 3, গলনাঙ্ক), climbs to 100 °C (square 5), is **flat at 100 °C** (squares 5 to 7,
স্ফুটনাঙ্ক), then climbs again to 120 °C. Caption: "চিত্র ৬.০৮: তাপ প্রয়োগ করার সময় গলনাঙ্ক এবং স্ফুটনাঙ্কের তাপমাত্রার
পরিবর্তন হয় না।" The text around it: a solid heated rises in temperature; at the গলনাঙ্ক it melts (গলন) and the
temperature stops rising because the heat loosens the bonds between molecules (গলনের সুপ্ততাপ); once melted it rises
again until the স্ফুটনাঙ্ক, where বাষ্পীভবন happens (বাষ্পীভবনের সুপ্ততাপ); then the gas heats on (plasma is "অন্য
ব্যাপার"). The one example the book names is **বরফ, পানি এবং বাষ্প**. The opposite also happens when heat is taken away:
**ঘনীভবন (Liquification)**, gas to liquid, and **কঠিনীভবন (Solidification)**, liquid to solid (p176–177). The sweat
example (p176): in a hot, crowded place, stepping into the open air cools you because sweat evaporating takes the
বাষ্পীভবনের সুপ্ততাপ from the body.

Pages 177–178 go on to **বাষ্পায়ন (Evaporation)** and what it depends on (বাতাসের প্রবাহ, ক্ষেত্রফল, তরলের প্রকৃতি,
বাতাসের চাপ, উষ্ণতা, বায়ুর শুষ্কতা). That has no picture and is a different setup (a dish of water in the air, not a
heated beaker): it is a separate figure, not part of this one. 6.5 (আপেক্ষিক তাপ) starts on p178: not here.
The "নিজে করো" box at the top of p175 (two balloons in hot water) belongs to the topic before 6.4.

## The one idea to teach
While a solid is heated, its temperature rises, but **not while it is changing state**: at the গলনাঙ্ক (ice, 0 °C) and
the স্ফুটনাঙ্ক (water, 100 °C) the temperature stays flat and the heat goes into the সুপ্ততাপ. Taking heat away runs the
same curve backwards (ঘনীভবন, কঠিনীভবন).

## What the figure does
The book's graph hangs on a lab wall; beside it, the beaker it describes (ice, a spirit lamp under a tripod, a rod
thermometer). One numbered button plays the next of the five parts of the curve; a finger on the graph goes to any
moment. "তাপ সরানো" plays the curve the other way (drawn mirrored, time still left to right; lamp out).
The graph is the book's, so the time axis has no numbers and the two flat parts are the same length; real water's
latent heats are not in that ratio and the figure does not claim they are.
