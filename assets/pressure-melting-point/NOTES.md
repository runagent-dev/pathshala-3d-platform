# Notes: Pressure and melting point: a wire through ice (চাপের ফলে গলনাঙ্ক)

Book: physics-9-10 › 6.7 › p181–182 (reference pictures: `reference/page-181.png`, `reference/page-182.png`)

## What the page shows
Topic 6.7 "গলনাঙ্ক এবং স্ফুটনাঙ্কের ওপর চাপের প্রভাব (Effect of Pressure on Melting Point and Boiling Point)".
Page 181 starts the topic: "চাপ দেওয়া হলে পদার্থের গলনাঙ্ক কমে যায়, তাই দুই টুকরা বরফকে চাপ দিয়ে এক টুকরো বরফে পরিণত করে
ফেলা যায়…", where the pressure falls the melting point falls so the ice there melts at the ice's own temperature, and
when the pressure is removed the melting point returns and the water freezes into one block again. Page 182 has the
only picture, **চিত্র ৬.০৯**: a pale blue block of ice across a pink table, a thin wire over it with a weight hanging
on each side ("একটি বরফ খণ্ডকে সূক্ষ্ম তারের চাপ দিয়ে কাটা সম্ভব"): the wire seems to cut the block in two but the ice
is still one whole piece. The page then goes on, in words only, to boiling (mountain climbers' cooking, the pressure
cooker) and to gas (pressure raises the point at which a gas turns liquid), then the sample questions.

**This figure is only the book's picture (চিত্র ৬.০৯) and the melting-point paragraphs.** The boiling-point
paragraphs have no picture and are the second figure of this topic: `pressure-boiling-point`.

## The one idea to teach
Pressure lowers the melting point; when the pressure is removed the melting point comes back. So the ice under the wire
melts, the water above it freezes again, and the block is never cut.

## The sequence (one big button)
1. তার ও দুটি ওজন ঝুলিয়ে দাও: the wire and the two weights come down onto the block; the wire presses (one red arrow,
   "বেশি চাপ") and a bright film of water shows under it ("গলে পানি").
2. সময় এগিয়ে দাও: the wire sinks through (7 s, time sped up, said in a note); the faint seam above it is the water
   frozen again ("জমে বরফ"). At the end it looks as if the block were cut in two.
3. তার ও ওজন সরিয়ে নাও: the block is still one piece ("অখণ্ড বরফ").
Then "আবার দেখো". The quiet icon goes back to the start.

## Choices made
- The wire runs across the block's narrow side, with a weight at each end (as the book). The view is oblique like the
  book's, framed on the block, the wire and both hanging weights (the table is short and its top is two rails, so
  nothing hides the weights); dragging turns the view.
- 0.1.1: bigger block, thicker dark wire, at most three labels (ice, wire, weight); the film of water (blue) and the
  refrozen seam (pale teal) are named by the panel's two rows, the red arrow above the wire by the first row.
- No numbers anywhere (the book gives none): the speed is a picture and the panel says so. The wire is drawn thicker
  than a real one.
- The block is see-through pale blue (no `transmission`); the wire and arrow are drawn so they read inside it.
- The test hides what would give an answer away (the film of water in the first question, the seam until the answer).
