## What the page shows
Section ৩.২.২ তড়িৎ চৌম্বক বল, text only, no picture: combing hair and picking up bits of paper, magnets attracting
and repelling each other; electric and magnetic forces seem different but are the same force; far stronger than
gravity (10³⁶ গুণ); its range is infinite. This lab is the magnets half.

## The one idea to teach
An electric current makes a magnet (the coil), so electric and magnetic forces are one force, the electromagnetic
force: unlike poles attract, like poles repel, and the pull reaches across the whole track (its range is infinite).

## What the student does
One scene: a cell, a switch and a coil of copper wire on a bench, a bar magnet (N red, S grey) on a little cart.
One big button: "১ সুইচ চালু করো" (the coil pulls the cart in), "২ চুম্বকটি ঘুরিয়ে দাও" (pushed to the far end),
then "চুম্বকটি আবার ঘুরিয়ে দাও" (pulled in again from the far end). One outlined blue force arrow, the result line
and the rule. Test: the poles, switch off, which is stronger, the range.
