## The one idea to teach
A body wants to keep doing what it is doing: at rest it wants to stay at rest (স্থিতি জড়তা: the bus starts, the feet
go with it, the upper body stays, you lean back); moving it wants to keep moving (গতি জড়তা: step off a moving bus,
the feet stop, the upper body runs on, you tumble forward).

## Terms (as in the book)
জড়তা, স্থিতি জড়তা, গতি জড়তা, স্থির থাকতে চায়, গতিশীল থাকতে চায়, পেছনের দিকে হেলে পড়া, হুমড়ি খেয়ে পড়া,
শরীরের নিচের অংশ, শরীরের ওপরের অংশ. The book writes ওপরের three times in the স্থিতি জড়তা paragraph and উপরের once in
the next; the figure uses ওপরের throughout (the book's own form here, and the term list of this topic). The passenger
is "ছেলেটি / সে" everywhere (the book's "সে"); the book's general sentences keep "আমরা".

## Physics
- Bus: a(t) switched on and off over 0.08 s (sudden) or 1.5 s (gentle, 0.45 m/s²).
- Passenger: an inverted pendulum about the ankles, in the bus's frame:
  `I θ'' = m g L sin θ + m a L cos θ − K θ − C θ'`, I = 1.3 m L² (a body, not a point), L = 0.92 m (centre of mass),
  muscles K, C for a natural sway of ω = 2.8 rad/s with damping ratio 0.68 (little overshoot: he straightens up
  without rocking). The drawn pose spreads θ over the legs (0.7 θ) and the trunk (1.6 θ); the upper body's speed is
  that of the shoulders in the drawn pose. At the moment the poster shows (1.05 s): feet 1.3 m/s, shoulders 0.8 m/s.
- Stepping off: the feet stop; the upper body runs on at the speed it had (the legs give a little), so the body
  starts to turn about the feet at the rate that keeps the shoulders at that speed, then gravity tips it further,
  until the hands reach the road (then a short, drawn move to hands and knees, hands staying where they landed).
  The shoulders never go faster than the bus did (3.0 m/s, within 0.1 m/s while he springs from the step).
- Coin: kinetic friction μ = 0.25 while the card slides under it (≈ 25 ms), so the coin gets only ≈ 7 cm/s; it falls
  0.11 m into the glass (≈ 0.15 s) about 1 cm off centre.
- All of it is computed once per event with a fixed 1/240 s step (deterministic), unit-tested in
  `test/unit/inertia.test.mjs`.
