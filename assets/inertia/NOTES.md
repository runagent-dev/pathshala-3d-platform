## The one idea to teach
A body wants to keep doing what it is doing: at rest it wants to stay at rest (স্থিতি জড়তা: the bus starts, the feet
go with it, the upper body stays, you lean back); moving it wants to keep moving (গতি জড়তা: step off a moving bus,
the feet stop, the upper body runs on, you tumble forward).

## Terms (as in the book)
জড়তা, স্থিতি জড়তা, গতি জড়তা, স্থির থাকতে চায়, গতিশীল থাকতে চায়, পেছনের দিকে হেলে পড়া, হুমড়ি খেয়ে পড়া,
শরীরের নিচের অংশ, শরীরের ওপরের অংশ. The book writes ওপরের three times in the স্থিতি জড়তা paragraph and উপরের once in
the next; the figure uses ওপরের throughout (the book's own form here, and the term list of this topic). The passenger
is "ছেলেটি / সে" everywhere (the book's "সে"); the book's general sentences keep "আমরা".

  1. হঠাৎ চলা শুরু: 2.5 m/s² for 1 s (0 → 2.5 m/s). The feet go with the bus; the upper body lags; he leans back

     started: the head stays at it at first while the feet are carried forward. The event ends once he is upright.
  2. হঠাৎ ব্রেক: from 4.5 m/s at 3 m/s². The feet stop with the bus; the upper body runs on; he leans forward
     about 18° (গতি জড়তা). It ends once he is upright again.
  3. চলন্ত বাস থেকে নামা: the bus moves at 3 m/s; he hops down from the step of the rear door (0.4 m, 0.35 s in

     onto his hands and knees (গতি জড়তা). The road beside the bus has tar seams, patches and drain covers, so it
     visibly streams past before he steps; the view takes in the road in front of where he lands. The rear door is
     behind the rear wheel, so only the back of the bus passes him (no wheel rolls past a boy on the road); the
     camera then comes a little lower and closer.
  4. কয়েন ও কার্ড: a finger flicks a card from under a coin on a glass (card 2.2 m/s); the coin stays and drops into
     the glass (স্থিতি জড়তা). A home experiment, on a light tablecloth, seen from above.
- A blue arrow on the feet (or the card) and an orange arrow at the shoulders (or on the coin) show each part's
  speed along the road (0.28 m of arrow per m/s); the panel shows the two speeds in m/s (one decimal), the book's
  definition, and the event: before it, what is about to happen and what to watch; while it happens, the book's
  explanation; after the upper body has caught up (start, brake), what happened and how he straightens up again.
  Labels say what each part does ("নিচের অংশ: বাসের সাথে চলে", "ওপরের অংশ: স্থির থাকতে চায়", …) only while that is
  true: from the push until the upper body's speed reaches the feet's (start) or falls to it (brake); stepping off,
  from the landing until his hands are on the road. They are placed so they never cover the passenger, the arrows
  or the buttons.
- The camera moves on at the speed everything had before the push (an inertial frame) until a moment after it, so
  the student sees the upper body stay where it was while the feet are carried away (or stopped); then it follows the
  feet smoothly.
- Test (5 questions, in a new random order each round, each sets up its own scene with the arrows and labels
  hidden; after the answer the scene plays on and shows the answer, and a wrong answer gets a line naming its
  mistake): why one leans back on a start, or which way (start); why one leans forward on braking (inertia of
  motion; the other kind, the wrong part of the body, or a push), or which way (brake); which way one falls stepping
  off (asked while he is in the air); what the coin does; how much one leans if the bus starts gently.

## Physics
- Bus: a(t) switched on and off over 0.08 s (sudden) or 1.5 s (gentle, 0.45 m/s²).
- Passenger: an inverted pendulum about the ankles, in the bus's frame:
  `I θ'' = m g L sin θ + m a L cos θ − K θ − C θ'`, I = 1.3 m L² (a body, not a point), L = 0.92 m (centre of mass),
  muscles K, C for a natural sway of ω = 2.8 rad/s with damping ratio 0.68 (little overshoot: he straightens up
  without rocking). The drawn pose spreads θ over the legs (0.7 θ) and the trunk (1.6 θ); the upper body's speed is
  that of the shoulders in the drawn pose. At the moment the poster shows (1.05 s): feet 1.3 m/s, shoulders 0.8 m/s.
- Stepping off: the feet stop; the upper body runs on at the speed it had (the legs give a little), so the body
  starts to turn about the feet at the rate that keeps the shoulders at that speed, then gravity tips it further,
  until the hands reach the road (then a short, drawn move to hands and knees, hands staying where they landed).
  The shoulders never go faster than the bus did (3.0 m/s, within 0.1 m/s while he springs from the step).
- Coin: kinetic friction μ = 0.25 while the card slides under it (≈ 25 ms), so the coin gets only ≈ 7 cm/s; it falls
  0.11 m into the glass (≈ 0.15 s) about 1 cm off centre.
- All of it is computed once per event with a fixed 1/240 s step (deterministic), unit-tested in
  `test/unit/inertia.test.mjs`.

## Open questions
- The book's idealisation says the upper body is "তখনো স্থির"; in the model the head moves less than 1 cm from its
  mark in the first 0.3 s (the feet: 8 cm) and 3 cm while the feet move 16 cm; then the shoulders follow at about
  half the feet's speed. The labels
  say "স্থির থাকতে চায়" (wants to stay at rest), which is true; the numbers show the lag honestly.
- The 4th button (coin and card) is not in the book's text; it is the classic home experiment for স্থিতি জড়তা.
