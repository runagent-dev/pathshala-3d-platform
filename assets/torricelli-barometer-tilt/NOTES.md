# Notes: Torricelli's tube tilted (হেলানো টরিসেলির নল)

Book: physics-9-10 › 5.4.1 › p146. Screenshot: `reference/page-145.png` (both pages). The owner's picture:
`reference/owner-barometer.png`. Split from `torricelli-barometer` (1.0.0), where it was the third of six scenes.

## What the page shows
চিত্র ৫.১০: Torricelli's tube in a dish of mercury; the mercury stops at 76 cm with a vacuum above. The page does not
tilt the tube; this lab checks the idea behind P = hρg (topic 5.3): only the vertical height counts.

## The one idea to teach
Tilt the tube and the mercury fills more of it, but its top stays level with 76 cm on the scale: the air's pressure
holds up a column of a certain vertical height, not length.

## What the student does
One object, one button, one idea (the owner: "too much; should be easier"). নলটি আরও হেলাও tilts the tube 10° at a
time to 40° (then আবার খাড়া করো; arrow keys step too). A dashed orange line runs from the mercury's top to the scale;
the vertical height is drawn in orange; one outlined blue air-pressure arrow presses on the dish. At 40° a thin vacuum
is still left (tube 105 cm inside). The panel: one short line, the vertical height and the length along the tube
(76 / cos θ), the rule once the student has tilted it. Test: 3 questions (the vertical height at two tilts, read off
the scale; why it stays the same).
