# Notes: A barometer up a mountain (পাহাড়ে ব্যারোমিটার)

Book: physics-9-10 › 5.4.1 › p146. Screenshot: `reference/page-145.png` (both pages). The owner's picture:
`reference/owner-barometer.png`. Split from `torricelli-barometer` (1.0.0), where it was the fourth of six scenes.

## What the page shows
Torricelli's tube (চিত্র ৫.১০) stops at 76 cm; "বাতাসের চাপ মাপার যন্ত্রের নাম ব্যারোমিটার". Topic 5.4: the air's
pressure comes from the column of air overhead (বাতাসের স্তম্ভ).

## The one idea to teach
Higher up there is less air overhead, so less air's pressure, and the barometer's column is lower.

## What the student does
One object, one button, one idea (the owner: "too much; should be easier"). আরও উঁচুতে নাও takes the barometer to
1000, 2000 and 3000 m (then সমুদ্রতলে ফেরাও; arrow keys step too). The window's view changes from plains to snowy
peaks, the one outlined air-pressure arrow on the dish shortens and the column falls to about 67, 60 and 53 cm (the
standard atmosphere, scaled to the book's 76 cm at the sea; "প্রায়" on screen). The panel: one short line, the height
and the column, the rule once the student has gone up. Test: 3 questions (what happens higher up, the column read off
the scale, why).

## Terms (as in the book)
ব্যারোমিটার, পারদ স্তম্ভ, শূন্যতা, বাতাসের চাপ, বাতাসের স্তম্ভ, সমুদ্রতল.

## Look
A plain school lab (a warm teak bench, a blue-grey wall), never white; the window's view shows the height.

## Open questions
