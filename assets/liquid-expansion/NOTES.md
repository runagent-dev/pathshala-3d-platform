# Notes: Expansion of liquids (তরল পদার্থের প্রসারণ)

Book: physics-9-10 › 6.3.2 › pages 171–173 (the heading is at the foot of p171, the pictures are on p172, the
section ends on p173 where 6.3.3 gas expansion starts and is **not** part of this figure).

## What the pages show
- **p171** (heading only; the worked solids examples above it belong to 6.3.1): "৬.৩.২ তরল পদার্থের প্রসারণ": a liquid has only
  volume, so its expansion means its volume expansion.
- **p172, Fig 6.05**: the fever thermometer, a thin glass tube with a bulb at the foot holding mercury (পারদ), a narrow
  bend / constriction (সরু বক্রতা) just above the bulb. Caption: the fine bend is made so the mercury cannot fall back.
  Text: heat makes the mercury's volume grow and climb a very narrow tube; how far it climbs is the temperature; after the
  temperature falls the column cannot come down by itself: "ঝাঁকিয়ে নামাতে হয়".
- **p172–173, Fig 6.06 (heading "প্রকৃত এবং আপাত প্রসারণ")**: a round-bottomed glass flask with a narrow neck, marks C (top),
  A (middle), B (low) on the neck. Filled to A and heated: the level first falls to B (the bulb expands first), then
  rises past A to C (the liquid expands more than glass). AB × the tube's cross-section = the container's expansion V_B,
  BC × cross-section = the liquid's real expansion V_L, and **V_a = V_L − V_B** is the apparent expansion (what you would
  call the expansion if you forgot the container).
- Fig 6.07 (gas in a cylinder with a piston) is topic 6.3.3: left to the neighbouring figure.

## The one idea to teach
A liquid is always in a container that expands too; so the level first falls (container) and then rises (liquid, which
expands more), and the rise you see (apparent) is the real expansion minus the container's.

## What the figure does
One figure, two words to choose from (the page's two pictures), each a short guided sequence with one big step button:
- **থার্মোমিটার** (Fig 6.05): 1 গরম পানিতে রাখো (the mercury climbs to 39.0 °C) → 2 বের করে নাও (the thermometer comes
  out, the column is cut at the narrow bend and stays at 39.0 °C) → 3 ঝাঁকাও (it is shaken and the column falls) → আবার.
- **প্রকৃত ও আপাত প্রসারণ** (Fig 6.06): 1 বাল্বটি গরম করো (level A → B, teal bar AB = V_B) → 2 আরও গরম করো (B → C,
  orange bar BC = V_L) with V_a = V_L − V_B worked out in the panel → আবার.
- Test: 5 questions (first move of the level; which part is V_B; V_a from AB, BC and the area; what the column does when the
  thermometer is taken out; how to bring it down), every wrong choice a real mistake with its own note.

## Choices that are not on the page (flagged)
- A spirit lamp under the flask and a beaker of warm water for the thermometer: the heat sources, needed to show "তাপ দেওয়া হলে".
  The stand and clamp hold the glass. No other props.
- The numbers: neck cross-section 2 cm², AB = 1.5 cm, BC = 3 cm (so V_B = 3, V_L = 6, V_a = 3 cm³). The book gives no numbers
  for this picture; the marks are drawn far apart so they can be seen (a real bulb's expansion is much smaller), and the
  glass is drawn about 5 % bigger when heated. The test varies AB and BC.
- The thermometer's scale is 35–42 °C (a real fever thermometer's); the page prints no scale.
- The book treats B as the level after the container has expanded and before the liquid starts; the figure does the same
  (the glass is heated first, then the liquid), a simplification of what really happens together.
