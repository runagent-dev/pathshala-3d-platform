# Notes: Digital balance (ডিজিটাল ব্যালান্স)

Book: physics-9-10 › 1.6.2 › p23

## What the page shows
Section ১.৬.২ ব্যালান্স (ভর মাপার যন্ত্র). The text: mass cannot be measured directly, so usually the weight is
measured and the mass found from it; "1 kg" of something means its mass, not its weight; the old নিক্তি compared
the mass with বাটখারা; today electronic balances are common, and the balance's sensor gives the mass as soon as
the thing is put on it. Photo চিত্র ১.০৯ ডিজিটাল ভর মাপার যন্ত্র: a square steel pan on a black body with a yellow
console, a long display and blue and red buttons (the figure copies this look). Screenshot: `reference/page-23.png`.

## The one idea to teach
A digital balance shows mass directly: put a thing on the pan and read the display. It actually senses the weight
(W = m × g), and the "শূন্য করো" (tare) button lets you weigh something in a bowl without the bowl.

## Terms (as in the book)
ভর, ওজন, ব্যালান্স, ডিজিটাল ভর মাপার যন্ত্র, গ্রাম, কিলোগ্রাম, নিউটন, অভিকর্ষজ ত্বরণ (g = ৯.৮ মি/সে²).

## How it works
- Tap a thing on the table (ডিম, আম, পয়সা, ১০০ গ্রাম বাটখারা, বাটি) to put it on the pan; tap again to take it off.
  With the bowl on the pan, the others go into the bowl. Up to three things.
- The display settles for a moment, then shows "স্থির" (stable). Least count 0.1 g, capacity 1000 g.
- শূন্য করো (tare), একক (g ↔ kg) and ভেতরে দেখো (see the load cell inside, with the weight arrow W) are on the
  console and in the panel.
- The panel shows ওজন ÷ g = ভর with the numbers of the thing on the pan.
- Test (৫ questions, tap answers): read a kg display in grams; bowl tared, then a thing: what does the display
  show; given the weight in newtons, what mass will the display show.
