# Notes: Acceleration (ত্বরণ)

Book: physics-9-10 › 2.6 › p43 (the section runs onto p44)

## What the page shows
Section ২.৬ ত্বরণ (Acceleration), text, an উদাহরণ and one picture. Text: a body moving at সমবেগ has no ত্বরণ; whenever
the বেগ changes there is ত্বরণ: ত্বরণ হচ্ছে সময়ের সাথে বেগের পরিবর্তনের হার. As বেগ has both দিক and মান, it can change
in two ways: on a winding path, every turn changes the বেগ (ত্বরণ) even at সমদ্রুতি; a stone whirled on a string
(চিত্র ২.০৫) keeps changing direction, so it accelerates. In সরলরৈখিক গতি there is no change of direction: the ত্বরণ
comes only from the change of বেগের মান (দ্রুতি); বেগ বাড়লে বেগের দিকে ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ, কমলে ঋণাত্মক ত্বরণ বা মন্দন
(deceleration). ত্বরণ একটি ভেক্টর রাশি. উদাহরণ: চিত্র ২.০৬ (সময়ের সাথে বেগের পরিবর্তন), a বেগ–সময় graph with four pieces:
A rising (ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ), B flat (ত্বরণ নেই), C rising, D falling (মন্দন বা নেগেটিভ ত্বরণ). Page 44: only straight-line
motion from here on; for সমত্বরণ, ত্বরণ = (অতিক্রান্ত সময়ের শেষে বেগ − অতিক্রান্ত সময়ের আদিতে বেগ) / অতিক্রান্ত সময়, i.e.
a = (v − u)/t; মাত্রা [a] = LT⁻², একক ms⁻². Screenshot: `reference/page-43.png` (page 43 and the top of 44).

## The one idea to teach
ত্বরণ is the rate of change of বেগ: on a straight road, speeding up is ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ, slowing down is মন্দন, a steady
speed is no ত্বরণ (a = (v − u)/t); and because বেগ has a direction, turning at a steady speed is ত্বরণ too.

## Terms (as in the book)
ত্বরণ, সমবেগ, বেগের পরিবর্তনের হার, দিক, মান, দ্রুতি, সমদ্রুতি, ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ, ঋণাত্মক ত্বরণ, মন্দন, সমত্বরণ, আদি বেগ,
শেষ বেগ, সময়, ভেক্টর রাশি; m/s, s, ms⁻² (the book writes ms⁻² for ত্বরণ; বেগ in m/s, as in 2.5).

## How it works
- A CNG auto-rickshaw (green body, dark roof, grill doors, a driver) on a Bangladeshi road: asphalt with a dashed
  centre line, black-and-white kerbs, trees behind (only behind, so nothing passes in front of it). Real size, real
  time: the camera follows the CNG and the road streams past.
- Two examples, two chips in the panel: **সোজা রাস্তা** (first) and **বাঁক**. Views: পাশ থেকে (side on) / উপর থেকে (on
  the bend) and ঘুরিয়ে দেখো (orbit).
- **সোজা রাস্তা**: two pedals on the picture (bottom corners, where the thumbs are when the phone is held sideways),
  press and hold, acting as the finger goes down and stopping as it lifts: **গতি বাড়াও** (+2 ms⁻²) and **ব্রেক**
  (−3 ms⁻²); nothing pressed keeps the speed (and the brake wins if both are held). Keys: hold ↑/→ and ↓/←. Top speed
  15 m/s (54 km/h). Over the CNG: the blue **বেগ** arrow (its length is the speed), and the **ত্বরণ** arrow: forwards and
  purple while speeding up, backwards and orange (**মন্দন**) while braking, gone (label **ত্বরণ নেই**) at a steady
  speed, standing, or at top speed. A speedometer (দ্রুতি, m/s) at the top left.
  The motion runs in ticks of 0.1 s with a constant change per tick (সমত্বরণ), so every number shown is exact.
- The panel draws the **বেগ–সময় graph** (0–30 s, 0–15 m/s) as the CNG goes, starting with the first press of the gas.
  Each piece (a run of one kind) gets a pale band and a colour (purple / grey / orange) and, once it lasts 1 s, a
  letter A, B, C… as in চিত্র ২.০৬. Under it, **a = (v − u) ÷ t** worked out live for the piece being driven
  ([C] ত্বরণ a = (১২.৪ − ৮.০) m/s ÷ ২.২ s = +২.০ ms⁻², ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ): v − u and t both grow while the gas is held, the
  ratio stays 2. Touch a piece of the graph to see its sum (and a sentence about it). After 30 s the graph is full and
  the picture stops; আবার শুরু starts a new trip. One sentence under the sum says what is happening and why.
- **বাঁক**: the same CNG on an open field of mown grass at a steady 4 m/s; **বাঁয়ে** and **ডানে** (held) turn it on a
  circle of 6 m. The speedometer stays at ৪.০, but the বেগ arrow turns (and a compass in the panel shows its direction,
  with a compass word); while it turns a purple **ত্বরণ** arrow points to the middle of the turn (v²/r ≈ 2.7 ms⁻², no
  number shown: the book has no formula for it), and its track on the grass is purple; going straight, the track is
  grey and the label says ত্বরণ নেই. The panel: দ্রুতি সবসময় একই, বেগের দিক বদলাচ্ছে / একই থাকছে, ত্বরণ আছে / নেই.
- Test (৫ questions in a shuffled order, tap answers, the picture sets each one up; the pedals are hidden and keys
  ignored while it runs):
  1. a book-style বেগ–সময় graph (0–20 s) on a card over the picture, in one ink colour (colour would give it away):
     which piece is মন্দন / ত্বরণ নেই / ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ (asked only for a kind that appears once in that graph); after
     the answer it is coloured;
  2. a from u, v, t of a CNG speeding up, the CNG drawn with a faint u arrow and the v arrow, each labelled (wrong:
     the unit of বেগ, v ÷ t, (v − u) × t in m);
  3. braking: the sign (wrong: the sign lost, u ÷ t as if it stopped, v ÷ t); the মন্দন arrow appears with the answer;
  4. two CNGs from rest, one behind the other in the same lane, labelled with their time and final speed; the one
     ending faster gains speed more slowly: which has the bigger ত্বরণ (wrong: the faster one, the same, "cannot tell
     without the distance");
  5. the CNG going round at the same speed: is there ত্বরণ (wrong: no, the speed stays the same; no, the velocity
     does not change; yes, the speed grows). The ত্বরণ arrow and the coloured track are hidden until the answer.
  The worked answer leads with the answer, gives the sum with units and names the mistake made.
- Colours: বেগ blue; ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ (and the ত্বরণ of turning) purple; মন্দন orange; ত্বরণ নেই grey; in the 3D, the graph,
  the sum and the pedals. Never colour alone: letters, words and arrow directions say the same.

## Open questions
- The book writes the unit of ত্বরণ as ms⁻² and (in 2.5) the unit of বেগ as m/s: the figure keeps both.
- The pedal accelerations (+2 ms⁻², −3 ms⁻²) are a brisk CNG and firm braking; the bend is 4 m/s on a 6 m circle.
- Is "গতি বাড়াও" the right word for the accelerator for class 9? (Alternative: "এক্সিলারেটর".)
