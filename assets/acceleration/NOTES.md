# Notes: Acceleration (ত্বরণ)

Book: physics-9-10 › 2.6 › p43 (the section runs onto p44)

## What the page shows
Section ২.৬ ত্বরণ (Acceleration), text, an উদাহরণ and one picture. Text: a body moving at সমবেগ has no ত্বরণ; whenever
the বেগ changes there is ত্বরণ: ত্বরণ হচ্ছে সময়ের সাথে বেগের পরিবর্তনের হার. As বেগ has both দিক and মান, it can change
in two ways: on a winding path, every turn changes the বেগ (ত্বরণ) even at সমদ্রুতি; a stone whirled on a string
(চিত্র ২.০৫) keeps changing direction, so it accelerates. In সরলরৈখিক গতি there is no change of direction: the ত্বরণ
comes only from the change of বেগের মান (দ্রুতি); বেগ বাড়লে বেগের দিকে ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ, কমলে ঋণাত্মক ত্বরণ বা মন্দন
(deceleration). ত্বরণ একটি ভেক্টর রাশি. উদাহরণ: চিত্র ২.০৬ (সময়ের সাথে বেগের পরিবর্তন), a বেগ–সময় graph with four pieces:
A rising (ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ), B flat (ত্বরণ নেই), C rising, D falling (মন্দন বা নেগেটিভ ত্বরণ). Page 44: only straight-line
motion from here on; for সমত্বরণ, ত্বরণ = (অতিক্রান্ত সময়ের শেষে বেগ − অতিক্রান্ত সময়ের আদিতে বেগ) / অতিক্রান্ত সময়, i.e.
a = (v − u)/t; মাত্রা [a] = LT⁻², একক ms⁻². Screenshot: `reference/page-43.png` (page 43 and the top of 44).

## The one idea to teach
ত্বরণ is the rate of change of বেগ: on a straight road, speeding up is ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ, slowing down is মন্দন, a steady
speed is no ত্বরণ (a = (v − u)/t); and because বেগ has a direction, turning at a steady speed is ত্বরণ too.

## Terms (as in the book)
ত্বরণ, সমবেগ, বেগের পরিবর্তনের হার, দিক, মান, দ্রুতি, সমদ্রুতি, ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ, ঋণাত্মক ত্বরণ, মন্দন, সমত্বরণ, আদি বেগ,
শেষ বেগ, সময়, ভেক্টর রাশি; m/s, s, ms⁻² (the book writes ms⁻² for ত্বরণ; বেগ in m/s, as in 2.5).

## How it works
- A CNG auto-rickshaw (green body, dark roof, grill doors, a driver) on a Bangladeshi road: asphalt with a dashed
  centre line, black-and-white kerbs, trees behind (only behind, so nothing passes in front of it). Real size, real
  time: the camera follows the CNG and the road streams past.
- Two examples, two chips in the panel: **সোজা রাস্তা** (first) and **বাঁক**. Views: পাশ থেকে (side on) / উপর থেকে (on
  the bend: from above, tilted 42°, north up so the arrows are seen turning) and ঘুরিয়ে দেখো (orbit).
- **সোজা রাস্তা**: two pedals on the picture (bottom corners, where the thumbs are when the phone is held sideways),
  press and hold, acting as the finger goes down and stopping as it lifts: **গতি বাড়াও** (+2 ms⁻²) and **ব্রেক করো**
  (−2 ms⁻², so every speed is a multiple of 0.2 m/s and even a stop ends exactly on a tick); nothing pressed keeps the
  speed (the driver holds it; the brake wins if both are held). Keys: hold ↑/→ and ↓/←. Top speed 15 m/s (54 km/h).
  Over the CNG: the blue **বেগ** arrow (its length is the speed), and the **ত্বরণ** arrow: forwards and purple while
  speeding up, backwards and orange (**মন্দন**) while braking, gone (label **ত্বরণ নেই**) at a steady speed, standing,
  or at top speed. A speedometer (দ্রুতি, m/s) at the top left. The motion runs in ticks of 0.1 s with a constant
  change per tick (সমত্বরণ), so every number shown is exact.
- The panel draws the **বেগ–সময় graph** (0–15 m/s; the time axis grows 0–10, 0–20, 0–30 s with the trip) as the CNG
  goes, starting with the first press of the gas. Each piece (a run of one kind) gets a pale band and a colour (purple
  / grey / orange) and, once it lasts 1 s, a letter A, B, C… as in চিত্র ২.০৬ (a letter never moves to another piece).
  Under it, **a = (v − u) ÷ t** worked out live for the piece being driven ([C] ত্বরণ a = (১২.৪ − ৮.০) m/s ÷ ২.২ s =
  +২.০ ms⁻², ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ): v − u and t both grow while the gas is held, the ratio stays 2; under the sum, the book's
  sentence: ত্বরণ হচ্ছে সময়ের সাথে বেগের পরিবর্তনের হার। Touch a piece of the graph to see its sum (and a sentence about
  it). After 30 s the graph is full and the CNG rolls on at its last speed with the pedals let go; আবার শুরু starts a
  new trip. One sentence under the sum says what is happening and why.
- **বাঁক**: the same CNG on an open field of mown grass at a steady 2.5 m/s; **বাঁয়ে** and **ডানে** (held) turn it on a
  circle of 3 m (about the tightest a CNG can turn). The speedometer stays at ২.৫, but the বেগ arrow turns (and a
  compass in the panel shows its direction, with a compass word); while it turns, the circle it goes round is drawn
  dashed on the grass with its middle marked (বাঁকের কেন্দ্র: a dot on the ground and, on a thin stalk, a marker at the
  arrows' height with a dashed radius out to the CNG, so the arrow lines up with the marker on screen in every view; a
  level arrow over the roof would otherwise aim above a mark on the ground), a purple **ত্বরণ** arrow lies along that
  radius and points at the marker (v²/r ≈ 2.1 ms⁻², no
  number shown: the book has no formula for it), and its track on the grass is purple; going straight, the track is
  grey and the label says ত্বরণ নেই. The camera looks half-way between the CNG and the middle of its turn, so both are
  in the picture. The labels there find a free spot every frame (past or beside the arrow tips, round the CNG), clear
  of the buttons, the speedometer, the pedals, the CNG and each other. The panel: দ্রুতি সবসময় একই, বেগের দিক
  বদলাচ্ছে / একই থাকছে, ত্বরণ আছে / নেই.
- Test (৫ questions in a shuffled order, tap answers, the picture sets each one up; the pedals are hidden and keys
  ignored while it runs):
  1. a book-style বেগ–সময় graph (0–20 s) on a card over the picture, in one ink colour (colour would give it away):
     which piece is মন্দন / ত্বরণ নেই / ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ (asked only for a kind that appears once in that graph); after
     the answer it is coloured;
  2. a from u, v, t of a CNG speeding up or braking (half the time each), drawn with a faint u arrow and the v arrow,
     labelled আদি বেগ u / শেষ বেগ v (wrong, speeding up: the unit of বেগ, v ÷ t, (v − u) × t in m; braking: the sign
     lost, u ÷ t as if it stopped, v ÷ t); the ত্বরণ or মন্দন arrow, at its real length, appears with the answer;
  3. a CNG cruising at a steady speed (the speedometer never moves): its ত্বরণ (wrong: the speed itself, v ÷ t, v × t);
  4. two CNGs from rest, one behind the other in the same lane, labelled with their time and final speed: which has the
     bigger ত্বরণ. The pairs are mixed: the quicker one ends slower but gains speed faster; the quicker one gains speed
     more slowly; or both the same (দুটির সমান is then right), so neither "the faster one" nor "the quicker one" always
     wins (wrong choices also include "cannot tell without the distance");
  5. the CNG going round at the same speed: is there ত্বরণ (wrong: no, the speed stays the same; no, a change of
     direction does not change the velocity; yes, the speed grows). The ত্বরণ arrow, the circle and the coloured track
     are hidden until the answer.
  The worked answer leads with the answer, gives the sum with units and its middle step ((৩ − ১২) m/s ÷ ৩ s = −৯ m/s ÷
  ৩ s) and names the mistake made.
- Colours: বেগ blue; ধনাত্মক ত্বরণ (and the ত্বরণ of turning) purple; মন্দন orange; ত্বরণ নেই grey; in the 3D, the graph,
  the sum and the pedals (words in darker shades of the same, readable on white). Never colour alone: letters, words
  and arrow directions say the same.

## Open questions
- The book writes the unit of ত্বরণ as ms⁻² and (in 2.5) the unit of বেগ as m/s: the figure keeps both.
- The pedal accelerations (+2 ms⁻², −2 ms⁻²) are a brisk CNG and gentle braking; the bend is 2.5 m/s on a 3 m circle.
- Is "গতি বাড়াও" the right word for the accelerator for class 9? (Alternative: "এক্সিলারেটর".) The brake pedal says
  "ব্রেক করো" to match its verb form.
- The figure says গ্রাফ on screen (as the book does in the উদাহরণ); the aria label says লেখচিত্র.
