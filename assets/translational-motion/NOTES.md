# Notes: Translational motion (চলন গতি)

Book: Physics 9–10 (পদার্থবিজ্ঞান), chapter 2 (গতি), page 36, topic 2.2, the third of three paragraphs on the page
(after সরলরৈখিক গতি and ঘূর্ণন গতি). Screenshot: `reference/page-36.png`.

## What the page shows
Text only, no picture for this paragraph:

> চলন গতি (Translational Motion): কোনো কিছু যদি এমনভাবে চলতে থাকে যেন বস্তুর সকল কণা একই সময় একই দিকে যেতে
> থাকে তাহলে সেটা হচ্ছে চলন গতি। আমরা আমাদের চারপাশে মাঝে মাঝে এরকম অনেক উদাহরণ দেখতে পাই। গাড়ির ঘূর্ণায়মান চাকা
> বিবেচনায় না আনলে সোজা এগিয়ে যাওয়া একটা গাড়ির গতি চলন গতির উদাহরণ; গাড়ির প্রতিটি বিন্দু একই সময় একই দিকে একই
> দূরত্ব অতিক্রম করে।

## The one idea to teach
In চলন গতি every particle (every point) of the body moves the same distance in the same direction at the same time.

## Terms (as in the book)
চলন গতি, কণা, একই সময়, একই দিক, একই দূরত্ব, অতিক্রম করে, ঘূর্ণায়মান চাকা, বিবেচনায় না আনলে, বিন্দু, সোজা এগিয়ে
যাওয়া গাড়ি. Units: মিটার (the chapter uses দূরত্ব for distance; সরণ comes later). Spelling as the book: উপরে.

## How the figure works
- The book's own example, made visible: a real-size family car (4 m long, wheelbase 2.4 m, wheels 0.637 m across, so
  one turn of a wheel = 2 m) on a road with metre marks (numbered from where the rear bumper starts). Four painted
  points (stickers) at four clearly different heights, so no two trails run close together: সামনের বাম্পার
  (0.49 m), পেছনের দিক (0.72 m, the rear corner by the tail lamp), জানালা (1.15 m, the front window), ছাদ (1.475 m).
  One hue each (dark on white labels and the panel, at least 4.5:1; bright, with a dark outline, for the trails in the scene), and their names on labels while the car stands at the
  start (colour is never the only way to tell them apart).
- The student drags the car along the road (or taps it, or presses চালাও; arrow keys too). Each point leaves a
  trail with an arrowhead from a ring where it started; a faint outline stays where the car started. The panel
  reads how far each point went: straight ahead `৪.০ = ৪.০ = ৪.০ = ৪.০` and the verdict ✓ চলন গতি. The verdict and
  the explanation appear only after 0.5 m (the first-use hand moves the car 0.35 m, so it never gives it away).
- The two conditions in the book's sentence become the two contrasts, as on/off buttons beside চালাও:
  - **চাকার বিন্দু** (ঘূর্ণায়মান চাকা): a point on the front tyre's tread edge traces a true cycloid: it touches the
    road, stops for an instant, rises 0.64 m, comes down after each 2 m, and goes 16 × radius = 5.09 m (৫.১) while
    the body goes 4 m. It starts on the road, so for the first 0.72 m it has gone *less* than the body; the panel
    says "বেশি পথ গেছে" only once it is ahead. The verdict reads ✓ চলন গতি (চাকা বাদে). The camera turns more to the
    side (on a tall phone it frames the front wheel's path, each arch about 120 px wide); the body's trails turn
    lighter so the arches stand out. The tyre point's marker faces the camera and is drawn over the road, like its
    trail. The tyre point belongs to the straight road (seen from above on the bend it is a meaningless sawtooth).
  - **বাঁকা পথে** (সোজা এগিয়ে যাওয়া): the same car takes a bend (rear axle radius 5 m, a quarter turn), turning as it
    goes; each point follows its own circle: ১০.৪ ≠ ৯.৪ ≠ ৮.০ ≠ ৯.৩ মিটার, ✗ চলন গতি নয়. Same car, same points, only
    the motion changes: চলন গতি is about how a body moves, not what it is.
- The camera is a long lens from far away (field of view 10°), almost a parallel projection, so equal trails look
  equal on screen wherever they are. It fits the car's real path (sampled points, not a box round it) to the frame's
  shape: from the front corner on tall phones, more from the side on wide frames, from higher up for the bend.
- Speeds are a car creeping off (top speed 1.6 m/s straight, 2.2 m/s round the bend) with a gentle start and stop;
  real time, not slowed down.
- Every request (button, drag, key) gets a number; a drive that waits for a camera move starts only if nothing newer
  came, even when a phone turning mid-move cuts the camera move short.

## The test (5 questions, 4 tap choices each, a worked answer naming the student's own mistake)
1. `dist` (always first): the car drives d m; "the front bumper's point went from the 3.8 m mark to the 6.3 m mark;
   how far did the window's point go?" Wrong choices: the end mark, the start mark, where the asked point ends up.
   Worked answer: 6.3 − 3.8 = 2.5 m, every point the same.
2. `which`: which of these is (or is not) চলন গতি, with look-alike pairs so no word gives it away: ড্রয়ার টেনে খোলা ✓
   vs দরজা খোলা ✗ (near the hinges it hardly moves), ঢালু তক্তায় বাক্সের পিছলে নামা ✓ vs বলের গড়িয়ে নামা ✗, লিফটের
   উপরে ওঠা ✓, বাঁক নেওয়া গাড়ি ✗, চলন্ত সাইকেলের চাকা ✗. The look-alike of the right answer is always among the choices.
3. `wheel`: which path does this point on the wheel take (a label points at it); the worked answer drives the car and
   draws the arches. Wrong choices: straight with the car (the book's warning), a circle in one place (the view from
   inside the car), looping round (it never goes back: it stops for an instant on the road).
4. `turn`: the car takes the bend; is it চলন গতি? Wrong choices include "no, because the wheels turn" (they turn going
   straight too). If answered mid-drive, the bend is finished so the numbers are the end's.
5. `same`: the roof's point and the front bumper's point (labelled) of a car going straight: same time, same
   direction, same distance ✓ vs the roof goes farther / the front arrives first / different directions.

## Bridge
`get` → `{ path, distance, distances: { front, window, roof, back, wheel }, translational, wheel, playing, view, mode }`
(metres, 2 decimals; `distance` is how far the rear axle has gone; `translational` is true on the straight road).
`set` takes `path` (the bend hides the tyre point), `distance` (with `animate: false` it jumps, otherwise the car
drives there), `wheel` (the straight road), `playing`, `view`. Events: `change`, `drive`, `path`, `view`,
`dragstart`, `dragend`, `practice-*`. GLB: the car with its painted points (metres).

## Visual update (v0.2.0)
- Requested treatment: deep blue scene, pearl-white car, restrained red accents, dark asphalt and warm ivory metre marks. The student panel stays white. Explicit white/transparent captures remain supported.
- The unbranded four-door family car now has a shaped painted body, crowned roof, glazed windows with seals and pillars, door gaps and handles, mirrors with indicators, projector lamps, LED bars, grille, plates, bumpers and exhaust tips. Seats, head restraints, dashboard and steering wheel are visible through the windows.
- Four treaded tyres have split-spoke alloys, brake discs, wheel bolts and red calipers. Calipers stay fixed to the steering hubs while tyres and wheels rotate.
- The original dimensions, axle centres, rolling radius, marker coordinates and motion equations remain authoritative. Bright trails retain the marker hue, names and numeric distances for recognition without relying on colour alone.
- A Bangladeshi vehicle (CNG, bus) was considered, but the book uses গাড়ি.

## Polish pass (2026-11-01, v0.3.0)
- **Setting.** The flat deep-blue backdrop is now a town street on an afternoon (`src/setting.js`): textured asphalt,
  raised concrete kerbs, paved footpaths in a running bond, compacted earth beyond, a red-brick boundary wall with
  plastered pillars and copings, trees and street lamps behind it, a row of three- and four-storey houses with
  windows and balconies, a sky dome and haze. The bend has its own version (kerbs and footpaths follow the curve; the
  wall, trees and houses leave a gap where the road turns through). Nothing tall stands between the camera and the
  car or its trails. A transparent embed (`bg=transparent`) still shows only the road and the car.
- **Arrows and labels.** Every trail, arrowhead and start ring has a dark outline (an inverted hull, sharing the
  trail's draw range), and the trails are a little thicker: they read on the white car, the dark road and the
  footpath alike. The metre numbers have a dark outline too. The window and roof colours are darker (#237a35,
  #c2410c) so every label chip and panel number is at least 4.5:1 on white. The points' names choose the first clear
  spot from a list (kit annotations `spots`/`avoid`): clear of each other, the corner buttons, the hint and the metre
  numbers; on short phones they are a little smaller so all four fit (at 390×520 the roof's name sat under the
  corner buttons).
- **Layout.** Already the standard (3D on top, the panel beside it only in short, wide frames). The test's first
  question was 3 lines on a phone, so the 3D view shrank by 23 px during it: it is shorter now (2 lines), and the
  test sheet is a little lower on tall phones, so the 3D view has 390 of 700 px (56 %, was 376) and keeps that size
  in every state and question. No panel scrolls at any of the six sizes, with the longest strings.
- **Performance, detail kept.** The car's static parts are merged into one mesh per material and each wheel into
  one mesh per material (geometries shared by the wheels on a side); rounded boxes use 2–3 corner segments; the
  start outline is made from the car's own merged geometries (nothing built twice); the kit's unused instrument
  materials (a brushed-steel canvas) are no longer made. The street is merged too and unlit (its light painted into
  vertex colours), drawn after the road so only visible pixels are shaded: five draw calls per path. Only the car
  casts a shadow, so the shadow box is fitted round the car and follows it: a 1024 map over 6.4 m (sharper than the
  2048 map over 18 m before). The metre marks are one mesh, the numbers one mesh of camera-facing quads on one
  texture (were 20 meshes and 10 textures). Front view: no pinch or wheel zoom (it broke the camera's fit).
