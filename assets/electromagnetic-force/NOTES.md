# Notes: Electromagnetic force (তড়িৎ চৌম্বক বল)

Book: physics-9-10 › 3.2.2 › p68 (`order: 2` of the four fundamental forces, 3.2.1–3.2.4)

## What the page shows
Section ৩.২.২ তড়িৎ চৌম্বক বল বা বিদ্যুৎ চৌম্বকীয় বল (Electromagnetic Force), text only, no picture: combing hair and
picking up bits of paper with the comb, magnets attracting and repelling each other; electric and magnetic forces
seem different but are the same force; it is far stronger than gravity (10³⁶ গুণ, ট্রিলিয়ন ট্রিলিয়ন ট্রিলিয়ন গুণ):
the whole Earth pulls the paper with all its mass, yet the comb's tiny charge wins; its range is infinite.
Screenshot: `reference/page-68.png` (3.2.1–3.2.3; this is the second section).

## The one idea to teach
Electric and magnetic forces are one force, the electromagnetic force, and it is enormously stronger than gravity:
a comb's tiny charge lifts a bit of paper against the pull of the whole Earth. Its range is infinite.

## How it works
- Two examples, two chips in the panel: **চিরুনি** (first, the book's own experiment) and **চুম্বক**.
- **চিরুনি**: a student (side view, light blue school shirt, as in speed-velocity) at a bench. The comb sits in the
  hair; the student drags it along the hair (or presses **চুল আঁচড়াও**: three passes). About every pass adds one
  charge level (0–5): blue "−" discs appear on the comb (two per level), the panel's আধান meter fills, and loose
  hairs on the crown stand up, leaning towards the comb (charged hair). On wide pictures the face is in frame too.
- **কাগজের কাছে আনো** flies the comb to the bench (the camera follows, low and close: the comb's right half, the bit
  with the arrows and both arrows; the comb's left end may run off the picture) and lowers it slowly over eleven
  bits of paper (8–13 mm, plausible torn bits). A bit jumps when the comb's pull is bigger than its weight:
  pull ÷ weight = (q/5)² × (45 mm / d)⁵ (a small polarisable bit in the comb's field: the pull grows very fast as the
  comb comes closer). So a fully charged comb lifts paper from 4.5 cm, a one-stroke comb from 2.4 cm, an uncharged
  comb never. The comb stops a little inside its reach (`stopHeight`), waits, and lifts the bits up to 5 cm, where
  they clearly hang in the air ("কাগজকে আকর্ষণ করে তুলে নাও"). The bench is narrow, so seen from low down its back
  edge stays below the lifted bits. The student can drag the comb up and down (and a little sideways).
- The bit with the arrows lies right under the comb's last teeth (inside the comb's span wherever it may be moved),
  so it is always the first to jump; it sticks at the comb's end, half beyond it, so its arrows stay clear of the
  comb. Its arrows: পুরো পৃথিবীর টান (down, purple, the মহাকর্ষ colour of 3.2.1, 28 mm) and চিরুনির টান (up, blue
  #1c7ed6, তড়িৎ চৌম্বক বল), drawn to the same scale until the comb’s is 2 times the weight (then it stays: close to
  the comb the real pull is much bigger still). The panel compares the two live (চিরুনির টান < / > পুরো পৃথিবীর টান,
  greyed with "–" while the comb is in the hair) and the idea line follows the same bit, so the two never disagree.
  Once the paper is up: "…সেই টানকে হারিয়ে দিল। বই বলছে, তড়িৎ চৌম্বক বল মাধ্যাকর্ষণ বলের চেয়ে 10³⁶ গুণ শক্তিশালী।"
  (two sentences, so it does not read as if this comb pulled 10³⁶ times the paper's weight).
  **আবার শুরু করো**: the bits fall back, the comb (charge 0) returns to the hair.
- **চুম্বক**: a circuit in a row on the bench: a 1.5 V cell (+ at its right end), a knife switch, a coil of copper wire
  on a plastic tube (no iron, so it is not attracted when off), and the wire back to the cell along the front.
  A bar magnet (N red, S grey: not blue, the force's colour) on a little cart. Main button **সুইচ চালু করো** (or tap
  the switch): amber chevrons run along the wires (the current, "তড়িৎ চলছে"; nothing runs while the switch is open),
  the coil's ends turn red/grey, named N and S under them: the coil has become a magnet, and the idea line says so
  (তড়িৎ আর চুম্বকের বল আসলে একই বল). Unlike poles attract (the cart rolls in and clicks against the coil), like poles
  repel (it rolls to the block at the end of the 6 cm track). Tap the magnet (or **চুম্বক উল্টাও**) to turn it round;
  drag it along the track.
- The forces: two equal and opposite blue arrows at the facing ends, just above them (a pull: from each body towards
  the gap, tips meeting there; a push: from the gap outwards), with the word আকর্ষণ / বিকর্ষণ right above the gap.
  The force falls off as 1/r⁴ (r between the middles), so to scale it would vanish within centimetres; the arrows are
  8 + 30·(F/F₀)^0.35 mm long, which keeps shrinking along the whole track but never reaches zero. At the end of the
  track the pull still beats the wheels' friction, so the magnet comes back: পাল্লা অসীম.
- The poles follow the right-hand grip rule: the current goes in at the coil's left end and round as a right-handed
  screw (its front turns carry current downwards), so the right end (facing the magnet) is N.
- Panel: the idea line (changes with the state, always the book's words), the live comparison, the strip of the four
  fundamental forces under the heading shared by the four figures, exactly "চার মৌলিক বল (সবচেয়ে দুর্বল → সবচেয়ে
  শক্তিশালী)", always on one small line: মহাকর্ষ, দুর্বল নিউক্লীয়, তড়িৎ চৌম্বক, সবল নিউক্লীয়, this one highlighted, each
  with its range (অসীম, 10⁻¹⁸ m, অসীম, 10⁻¹⁵ m), as an order only, never on a number scale; then the chips and the main
  button. On wide frames the comparison and the strip sit side by side.
- The labels in the picture place themselves every frame (src/labels.js): each tries a few spots and takes one that
  is inside the picture and clear of the corner buttons, the corner note, the other labels, the comb, the other bits
  of paper and the arrows; a label whose point is out of the picture is not shown. (Kit gap: the kit's
  createAnnotations pins labels at fixed offsets.)
- Test (5 questions, tap answers, the 3D sets each up and plays the outcome after the answer; the arrows only after
  the answer): why the paper rises (or: what an uncharged comb does, then it comes down and nothing jumps); like or
  unlike poles from the letters in the picture (a 2×2 of সমমেরু/বিপরীত মেরু × আকর্ষণ/বিকর্ষণ); the coil with the switch
  open (then it switches on and pulls); which force is stronger and by how much (তড়িৎ চৌম্বক/মাধ্যাকর্ষণ × 10³⁶/৩৬,
  without the 2 : 1 arrows, and the worked answer says the 10³⁶ is for like particles such as two protons); and, from
  the picture, whether the coil still pulls the magnet at the far end of the track. Wrong answers get a short note on
  the mistake (not on the strength question, whose worked answer is long already).
- Keys: space/enter the main button, 1/2 the examples, ← → comb along the hair / move the comb or the cart, ↑ ↓ the
  comb's height over the paper, x turns the magnet round, f/v views.

## Open questions
- The book's numbers for the four forces disagree: 3.2.2 says the electromagnetic force is 10³⁶ times gravity;
  3.2.4 says the strong force is 10⁴¹ times gravity and 100 times the electromagnetic force (which would make it
  10³⁸). The figures show the strengths as an order only (weakest to strongest), and quote each section's own
  sentence. 10³⁶ matches the electric ÷ gravitational force between two protons (1.24 × 10³⁶, unit-tested). For the
  book team.
- Stylised on purpose (a corner note in the magnet example says the first): the coil is an air-core coil fed by one
  cell, which in real life is a weak magnet; its pull on the cart is drawn about as strong as a small iron-core
  electromagnet's (the falling-off with distance is real). The force arrows are not to scale (see above). The comb's
  arrow is capped at 2 times the weight’s.
- Switch wording: "সুইচ বন্ধ" means "off" in everyday Bangla but could be read against "বন্ধ বর্তনী" (closed circuit),
  which the book teaches later; the figure says "সুইচ চালু করো" / "সুইচ খুলে দাও" and "সুইচ খোলা". Please check.
- "সুইচ" and "তারের কুণ্ডলী (কয়েল)" are not on page 68; please check they are the words the book uses later.
