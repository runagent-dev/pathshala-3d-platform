## The one idea to teach
The tip of a clock's second hand stays the same distance from the pin and goes round it on a circle: ঘূর্ণন গতি.

## Terms (as in the book)
ঘূর্ণন গতি, নির্দিষ্ট বিন্দু, সমদূরত্ব (সমদূরত্বে), বৃত্তাকার পথ, ঘড়ির কাঁটা. The panel quotes the book's definition word for
word, with its three key terms in the colours of the drawing: নির্দিষ্ট বিন্দু purple, সমদূরত্বে orange, বৃত্তাকার পথে blue.

## How the figure works
- A wall clock on a blue plastered wall; its second hand turns clockwise by itself. The fixed point (purple dot at
  the pin), a dashed orange line to a mark on the hand 12 cm from the pin, and the blue circular path it traces.
- The dashed line says "? সেমি" until the student measures: a touch on the moving point (or the turning hand)
  stops it under the finger and measures its distance; the number goes into the panel. Four measurements at four
  different places give the finding ("দূরত্ব সবসময় সমান!"). Dragging turns it round by hand; letting go measures there.
  দূরত্ব মাপো measures, থামাও/চালাও pauses and plays. Keys: arrows turn it, M measures, space plays/pauses, F/V the view.
- Honest stylisation, a quiet aside after the book's line in the panel: ছবিতে সময় দ্রুত চলে (the clock runs 6 times fast).
- Test (4 questions, shuffled), each with a worked answer:
  1. which of four lettered points is the fixed point (the minute-hand tip is a slow trap);
  2. the distance from the pin after half a turn or one turn (wrong answers from real mistakes);
  3. what does not change (distance from the fixed point vs position, direction, distance from the start);
  4. which everyday motion is circular (pinwheel tip or stone on a string vs straight-line motions).
