# Notes: Wave quantities (তরঙ্গ-সংশ্লিষ্ট রাশি)

Book: physics-9-10 › 7.2.3 › p195 (the section runs to p198). Page images in `reference/` (dev only).

## What the page shows
- p195, 7.2.3 "তরঙ্গ–সংশ্লিষ্ট রাশি": the quantities of সরল স্পন্দন গতি carry over to a periodic wave (পূর্ণ স্পন্দন,
  পর্যায়কাল, কম্পাঙ্ক, বিস্তার; a particle of the medium vibrates in সরল স্পন্দন). Two new ones: **তরঙ্গদৈর্ঘ্য**
  ("তরঙ্গের যেকোনো একটি দশা থেকে তার পরবর্তী একই দশার মাঝে দূরত্ব … অর্থাৎ এক পর্যায়কালে একটা তরঙ্গ যেটুকু দূরত্ব
  অতিক্রম করে") and **তরঙ্গের বেগ** (distance per second), with **v = fλ**. In another medium the speed and the
  wavelength change, "কিন্তু কম্পাঙ্কের বা পর্যায়কালের কখনো পরিবর্তন হয় না" (p196).
- উদাহরণ: চিত্র ৭.০৬ (displacement–distance: a = 0.1 m, λ = 2 m), ৭.০৭ (displacement–time: a = 0.2 m, T = 2 s),
  ৭.০৮ (both: a = 0.1 m, λ = 1 m, T = 0.2 s, f = 5 Hz, v = 1 m × 5 Hz = 5 ms⁻¹), ৭.০৯ (points A–E: A and C in the
  same দশা; A and B equal but opposite; D and E equal but not the same দশা).

## The one idea to teach
In one period T a wave moves on exactly one wavelength λ, so v = λ/T = fλ; on the same rope v stays the same and λ
changes with f; a different medium changes v and λ, never f.

## Terms (as in the book)
তরঙ্গ-সংশ্লিষ্ট রাশি, বিস্তার (a), তরঙ্গদৈর্ঘ্য (λ; the page also writes তরঙ্গ দৈর্ঘ্য), দোলনকাল / পর্যায়কাল (T),
কম্পাঙ্ক (f), তরঙ্গের বেগ (v), দশা, পূর্ণ স্পন্দন, সাম্যাবস্থা, মাধ্যম, বিচ্যুতি (m), দূরত্ব (m), সময় (s); units m, s,
Hz, m/s (the book writes ms⁻¹ in 7.08; the chapter's figures agreed on m/s).

## How the figure works
- **Scene** (metres, real proportions): a teak lab bench; a mechanical wave driver at x = 0 (powder-coated body,
  speaker grille, chrome drive shaft, brass clamp) fed by a signal generator whose display shows f; a 2 m braided
  rope over a graph board (0–2 m in tenths, ±0.1 m in 0.025 m squares, the rest line darker, a metre scale along the
  bottom); past 2 m the rope runs through two foam pads (they soak up the wave so nothing reflects: the wave is a pure
  travelling wave), over a pulley to a slotted mass. Slate-blue backdrop, no other scenery.
- **Physics**: y = a cos(kx − ωt), a = 0.1 m; the rope's surface (440 rings × 10 sides) is rebuilt in place each
  frame; the driver's shaft follows y(0, t); the green bead at x = 1.6 m only goes up and down. Light rope v = 5 m/s
  (the book's 7.08), heavy rope v = 2.5 m/s. Time is shown 10 times slower ("সময় ১০ গুণ ধীর", bottom-left).
- **Panel** (one action, one result, one rule): the row of words ৫ Hz (λ = 1 m) · ১০ Hz (0.5 m) · ২.৫ Hz (2 m) ·
  ভারী দড়ি (5 Hz, 0.5 m), with v under each once measured; the action "এক দোলনকাল চালাও" (or a tap on the driver):
  time runs exactly T, a crimson label counts t to T, a blue arrow follows the first crest, which ends exactly one λ
  on (the orange λ mark below), and a faint copy of the start shape shows the wave looks the same again. Quiet
  buttons: play/pause (the wave runs on; the marks hide while it moves) and start over. Two live graphs like
  চিত্র ৭.০৮ (displacement–distance with λ and a, displacement–time of the bead with T; the period just run drawn in
  crimson). Result: "T = ০.২ s সময়ে চূড়া গেল ঠিক λ = ১ m, তাই v = fλ = … = ৫ m/s". The rule grows: definitions of λ
  and a → "এক পর্যায়কালে … সেটাই তরঙ্গদৈর্ঘ্য, v = fλ" → same rope, twice f, half λ, same v → the book's medium
  sentence.
- **Test** (5): read a off the board; read λ off the scale (10 Hz, 2.5 Hz or the heavy rope); which of A–E share a
  phase (as চিত্র ৭.০৯, on the 10 Hz wave); v from the generator's f and the scale's λ; what doubling f does on the
  same rope. Each wrong choice is a real mistake with its own note.
- Keys: Space/Enter one period, ←/→ the examples, P play/pause, R start over.
- Colours (chapter 7): a green #2b8a3e, λ orange #e8590c, T crimson #c2255c, f grape #862e9c, v blue #1c6fd9.

## Honest simplifications
- Slowed 10 times (5 Hz shows as one cycle in 2 s). The pads that absorb the wave are idealised (no reflection at
  all); a real rope also sags a little and its wave loses some amplitude along the way.
- The board's 2 m is shorter than the book's 7.06 graph (4.5 m) so that a = 0.1 m is visible on a phone.
- At 390×520 (portrait, tightest) the graphs are hidden; a phone held upright is asked to turn sideways anyway.

## Open questions

- "দোলনকাল" (7.08) and "পর্যায়কাল" (7.2.3 text) are both used; the action says দোলনকাল, the rule quotes পর্যায়কাল.
- Words added that the page doesn't use: চূড়া (crest), খাঁজ (trough), চিহ্নিত কণা, ভারী দড়ি, সিগন্যাল জেনারেটর.
