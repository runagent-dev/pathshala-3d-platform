## The one idea to teach
Nothing ties the Moon to the Earth, yet it goes round the Earth's centre at about the same distance on a circle: ঘূর্ণন গতি.

## Terms (as in the book)
ঘূর্ণন গতি, নির্দিষ্ট বিন্দু, সমদূরত্ব (সমদূরত্বে), বৃত্তাকার পথ, চাঁদ, পৃথিবী. The panel quotes the book's definition word for
word, with its three key terms in the colours of the drawing: নির্দিষ্ট বিন্দু purple, সমদূরত্বে orange, বৃত্তাকার পথে blue.

## How the figure works
- The Moon goes round the Earth in a night sky of stars, anticlockwise seen from above the North Pole, always the
  same face to the Earth. The fixed point (purple dot at the Earth's centre), a dashed orange line to the Moon, and the
  blue circular path; the whole orbit is also drawn faintly from the start. About 3,84,400 km = 3.84 lakh km, always
  with "প্রায়" (the real orbit is slightly elliptical).
- The dashed line says "? লক্ষ কিমি" until the student measures: a touch on the moving point (or the turning Moon)
  stops it under the finger and measures its distance; the number goes into the panel. Four measurements at four
  different places give the finding ("দূরত্ব প্রায় সমান!"). Dragging turns it round by hand; letting go measures there.
  দূরত্ব মাপো measures, থামাও/চালাও pauses and plays. Keys: arrows turn it, M measures, space plays/pauses, F/V the view.
- Honest stylisation, a quiet aside after the book's line in the panel: আকার, দূরত্ব, সময় মাপমতো নয়.
- Test (4 questions, shuffled), each with a worked answer:
  1. what kind of motion the Moon's is (ঘূর্ণন, সরলরৈখিক, চলন, সরল স্পন্দন);
  2. where its fixed point is (Earth's centre, surface, the Moon's centre, halfway), the bodies named;
  3. what does not change (distance from the Earth's centre vs position, direction, distance from the start);
  4. which everyday motion is circular (pinwheel tip or stone on a string vs straight-line motions).
