## The one idea to teach
A stopwatch measures the time between starting and stopping it. Read it as minutes:seconds and hundredths, and
turn it into seconds: minutes × 60 + seconds (least count 0.01 s). Timing by hand adds a reaction delay that is
much bigger than the least count.

## How it works
- The watch looks like the photo. Press চালু/থামা on the watch (upper right) or "চালু করো / থামাও" in the panel to
  start and stop; শূন্য (upper left) or "শূন্য করো" resets it. Like a real stopwatch it does not reset while
  running (a hint says to stop first).
- The panel shows the time in seconds and the sum মিনিট × ৬০ + সেকেন্ড = মোট সেকেন্ড, and a line that reads the
  display out ("পর্দায় ৩:১১ ১২ মানে ৩ মিনিট ১১.১২ সেকেন্ড = ১৯১.১২ সেকেন্ড").
- বাতির সময় মাপো (the lamp game): a lamp appears next to the watch, lights after a moment for 2–5 s; the student
  starts the watch when it lights and stops it when it goes out, then sees the real time, their reading and the
  difference, with why (reaction time ≫ least count).
- Test (৫ questions, tap answers): read the display in seconds; the time between two readings (half the time across
  a whole minute); how a time in seconds looks on the display; the least count.
- Keys: space or s start/stop, r reset, l lamp game, f/v views.
