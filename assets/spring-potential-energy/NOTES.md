## The one idea to teach
A spring that is pushed in (or pulled out) and held still has no motion and no kinetic energy, yet the work done on it is not
lost: it is stored inside the spring as **বিভব শক্তি**, V = ½kx². The spring's force always points back toward its rest
position; it grows with x (F = kx), and the energy grows faster: twice x, twice the force, **four times** the energy.

## Stylised on purpose
- The ½ explanation (average force × distance) is not on the page; it follows the page's own argument (work done by a
  force becomes energy) and is offered only when the student taps ½. Open question 6 below.
- The bars under the formulas are not a graph (the page has none): two plain bars, full at the largest force and energy
  the figure can show.
- The spring hangs straight out from the board with no sag (a stiff spring), and its coils never touch (at 20 cm long the
  turns are still 15 mm apart).
- The hand holds the handle the same way to pull and to push (palm down, fingers round the bar); the book's picture pushes
  the bare end with a finger. An open palm pushing the end reached up through the x bracket on a phone.
- "আস্তে ছেড়ে দাও" brings the end back slowly with the hand; a spring let go suddenly would spring back and oscillate. The
  rule then says the stored energy went back to the hand.

## Open questions for the book team
1. The page treats only a **squeezed** spring; the owner's reference picture (and the figure) also pull it out. V = ½kx²
   holds for both. Is the stretched case welcome here, or should the figure keep to compression?
2. **F = kx** and the names F<sub>P</sub>, F<sub>S</sub> are not on the page (the page has only F). The figure shows F = kx
   in the result line; does the chapter introduce it elsewhere (Hooke's law), or should it be marked "বইয়ে নেই"?
3. The page gives no units; the figure uses SI (k in N/m, x in m, V in J) and shows x in cm on the ruler and the stepper,
   turned into metres in the sums. Is that right for class 9–10?
4. The page's "শক্তির সৃষ্টি হয়" (energy is created): the figure says the work is stored (জমা, সঞ্চিত) instead.
5. An older, unmerged branch `fig/potential-energy/first-version` (no pull request) has another figure for this page (a
   squeezed spring pushing a trolley, energy as cubes). This figure follows the owner's newer reference picture; the old
   branch can be closed if this one is taken.
6. The ½ explanation ("the force grows from ০ to kx, ½kx on average; work = average force × distance") is not on the
