## The one idea to teach

A force changes a body's momentum, and the rate of that change is proportional to the force and in its direction:
F = ma. The same force gives a light body a big acceleration and a heavy one a small one; a bigger force gives a bigger
acceleration; a force against the motion makes the momentum change backward (the body slows).
