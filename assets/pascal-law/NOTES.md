## What the page shows
Topic ৫.৩.৩ প্যাসকেলের সূত্র. A liquid passes on pressure in every direction; if it were not equal everywhere, the liquid
would flow "যতক্ষণ পর্যন্ত না চাপ সমান হয়ে যায়". The law in a box: "**একটা আবদ্ধ পাত্রে তরল বা বায়বীয় পদার্থে বাইরে থেকে
চাপ দেওয়া হলে সেই চাপ সমানভাবে সঞ্চালিত হয়ে পাত্রের সংলগ্ন গায়ে লম্বভাবে কাজ করবে।**" Then প্যাসকেলের সূত্রের ব্যবহার:
চিত্র ৫.০৫, two cylinders side by side joined by a pipe, cross-sections A₁ and A₂; a force F₁ on the first makes
P = F₁/A₁, passed on to the second, so F₂ = PA₂ = F₁(A₂/A₁). "(A₂/A₁) এর মান 100 হয় তাহলে … 100 গুণ বল পেয়ে যাচ্ছ!"
Used in "বড় বড় কলকারখানা কিংবা বিমান নিয়ন্ত্রণে". The caveat: "এটা বলবৃদ্ধিকরণ নীতি। এই পদ্ধতিতে শক্তি কিন্তু মোটেও বাড়ানো
যায় না। তুমি প্রথম সিলিন্ডারে যে পরিমাণ শক্তি প্রয়োগ করবে দ্বিতীয় সিলিন্ডারে ঠিক সেই পরিমাণ শক্তি ফিরে পাবে!" The
picture's caption: "F₁ বল প্রয়োগ করলে প্রস্থচ্ছেদের উপর নির্ভর করে অন্য দিকে F₂ বল পাওয়া যায়।"

playing with this. you will use proper color and all and very properly." The reference: a small piston pushed with

arrows and labels; Bangla first in the book's terms.

## The one idea to teach
The pressure a push makes in a closed liquid is the same everywhere (P = F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂), so a wider piston gets more
force, F₂ = F₁(A₂/A₁); but it moves as many times less (d₂ = d₁ A₁/A₂), so it multiplies force, not energy.

## Design
- A hydraulic car lift in a workshop: a red brick wall over a deep teal dado with an orange band, a pegboard of tools,
  a window of blue sky, a red tool chest, a stack of tyres, a blue oil drum, a blue-grey epoxy floor with yellow safety
  lines. The lift stands on a blue base plate, **cut open along its middle** ("সামনের অংশ কেটে দেখানো"): hatched steel
  walls, polished steel shells behind, golden hydraulic oil (amber at the bottom, light gold at the top; it was deep orange until the polish pass, too close to the red pressure arrows).
- The small (pump) cylinder: A₁ = 0.01 m² (a bore of 11.3 cm), a chrome rod and a T-handle with red grips, pushed
  straight down (no lever, so F₁ is the force on the piston). A thin pipe from the tank under the floor feeds it on the
  way up (a real lift's check valves are not drawn). The lift cylinder: A₂ = 10, 50 or 100 × A₁ (0.1, 0.5, 1 m²;
  three cylinders built, one shown), its ram carries a platform with diamond plate and a yellow-and-black edge, and a
  yellow hatchback on it. The two cylinders are true to each other (A₂/A₁ exact); the car is drawn smaller than life
  next to them, and the note says so ("গাড়ি ছোট করে আঁকা").
- Numbers: W = 8,000 N (a small car of about 816 kg; the platform's own weight left out). F₁ from 50 to 300 N (a
  person's push). With 100 × the car rises from 100 N; with 50 × from 200 N; with 10 × never (at most 3,000 N): the
  student finds that a bigger piston is needed. F₂ is never exactly W on this grid, so "more than" is never a tie.
- Arrows (book-notes colours where they exist): F₁ and F₂ orange (forces), W purple (weight's colour), the pressure red,
  the areas teal, the distances blue. Forces are thick, flat colour in a white outline with a dark rim, drawn over
  everything. F₂ pushes the platform up from below and W pushes it down from above, on one scale, so the longer one wins;
  F₂ and W use a smaller scale than F₁ (said in the rule). Pressure arrows are all the same length, each tip on the wall,
  pipe or piston it presses on, square to it: 18 of them on the cut face.
- One action: ① হাতলে চাপ দাও (the handle goes down 10 cm, the car rises 1 mm; or, F₂ < W, the handle shakes and nothing
  moves) → ② আরও ১০০ বার চাপ দাও (sped up, "সময় দ্রুত করে দেখানো"; the car rises 10 cm; up to 25 cm) → গাড়ি নামাও at the
  top. The first push takes the camera close to the oil (the equal red arrows), then to a millimetre scale on the cut of
  the lift cylinder's wall where the 1 mm rise shows (d₂), then back. The quiet eye button looks inside and back.
- The formula is the controls: the stepper F₁, the row of words A₂ = 10A₁ / 50A₁ / 100A₁, the law P = F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂
  (each symbol a button: its arrows or bar pulse), the sums P = F₁/A₁ = … Pa and F₂ = F₁(A₂/A₁) = … N with a green
  "F₂ > W" or red "F₂ < W" chip, and the car's weight.
- The rule grows in the book's words: the law's box → চাপ সব জায়গায় সমান, F₂ = F₁(A₂/A₁), and the book's "if not
  equal, it would flow" → বলবৃদ্ধিকরণ নীতি, শক্তি বাড়ে না with F₁d₁ = F₂d₂ = 10 J → কলকারখানা কিংবা বিমান নিয়ন্ত্রণে (after
  trying two pistons). F₂ < W gets its own rule (push harder, or a bigger piston).
- Changing F₁ or A₂/A₁ brings the car down (the valve opens); if the handle was being pushed, it is pushed again at once.
- Test: F₂ from F₁ and the pistons' areas on the picture (mistakes: F₂ = F₁, F₁ ÷ ratio, F₁ + ratio); how far the car
  rises for a push of 10/20/30 cm, the ratio read from A₁ and A₂ (d₁, d₁ × ratio, ÷ 10); the pressure under the big
  piston (× ratio, ÷ ratio, half); the direction of the oil's pressure on the walls (only down, along the walls, only
  towards the big piston); the energy out of one push (× ratio, ÷ ratio, cm not changed to m, half).

## Open questions
- The page has no numbers for A₁, F₁ or the car; 0.01 m², 50–300 N and 8,000 N are the figure's own (round numbers so
  the sums are easy). Real hydraulic lifts run at far higher pressures (MPa) with much smaller pistons.
- The 100 pushes are shown sped up, four pushes to each visible stroke of the handle.
- The check valves and the tank that let a pump push many times are not on the page (its machine pushes once); the
  figure draws only the tank's pipe and does not name it on screen (one idea at a time).
- "পিস্টন" is not on the page (it says সিলিন্ডার); the figure uses it for the part that moves.

    props (tyres, chest, drum) have soft contact shadows instead of casting, and the pistons (hidden in their

    redrawn for them); the tour's pauses are timers, not tweens, so a still view draws no frames while it waits. Label
