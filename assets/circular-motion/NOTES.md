## The one idea to teach
An object that keeps the same distance from a fixed point and goes round it on a circle is in ঘূর্ণন গতি.

## Terms (as in the book)
ঘূর্ণন গতি, নির্দিষ্ট বিন্দু, সমদূরত্ব (সমদূরত্বে), বৃত্তাকার পথ, বৈদ্যুতিক পাখা, ঘড়ির কাঁটা, চাঁদ, পৃথিবী.
The panel quotes the book's definition word for word, with its three key terms in the colours of the drawing:
নির্দিষ্ট বিন্দু purple (the dot at the centre), সমদূরত্বে orange (the dashed line and the measured numbers),
বৃত্তাকার পথে blue (the traced path).

## How the figure works
- Three chips switch between the book's examples: a ceiling fan seen from below (as from a bed), a wall clock's
  second hand, and the Moon going round the Earth. Each turns by itself (the right way: the fan anticlockwise seen
  from below, the clock clockwise, the Moon anticlockwise seen from above the North Pole, always the same face to
  the Earth).
- Each shows the fixed point (purple dot, labelled), a dashed orange line from it to the moving point with the
  distance written on it, and the blue circular path the point traces (labelled once half of it is drawn).
- The student touches the moving point (or anywhere on the turning blade/hand/Moon): it stops under the finger and
  its distance is measured. A thin line is left there and the number goes into the panel:
  `৭০ = ৭০ = ৭০ = ৭০`. After four measurements at different places the status says "দূরত্ব সবসময় সমান!".
  Dragging turns the example round by hand (the point can only move along its circle); letting go measures there.
  The panel's দূরত্ব মাপো measures on pointerdown; থামাও/চালাও pauses and plays. Keys: arrows turn it, M measures,
  space plays/pauses, 1/2/3 choose the example, F/V the view.
- The Moon: the dashed line is labelled "কোনো দড়ি নেই!" and a hint quotes the book's wonder when it is chosen.
- Honest stylisation, written in the corner of the 3D view: the fan is shown much slower (অনেক ধীর করে দেখানো),
  the clock faster (সময় দ্রুত করে দেখানো), and for the Moon sizes, distance and time are not to scale.
- Real numbers: a 56-inch (1400 mm) fan, the mark near the blade tip 70 cm from the axis; a 31 cm clock, the mark on
  the second hand 12 cm from the pin; the Moon about 3,84,400 km = 3.84 lakh km from the Earth's centre (shown as
  "প্রায়" in the test's explanation; the real orbit is slightly elliptical). The distances are measured on the
  model (the point's position relative to the fixed point), not typed in.
- Test (5 questions, order shuffled): which of four lettered points is the fixed point (the example keeps turning,
  so three letters go round and one stays; the minute hand's slow tip is a trap); the distance after half a turn or
  one turn (wrong answers: the distance from the start, half, double; the answer is then shown turning); what does
  not change (distance from the fixed point vs position, direction of motion, distance from the start); which
  everyday motion is circular (Ferris wheel or a stone on a string vs the book's straight-line examples); the Moon
  (what it does with nothing holding it, or where its fixed point is).
- Period (পর্যায়কাল) is not taught here: it is the next figure (page 37).

## Open questions

- The Earth's own spin is not shown (it would turn 27 times per lunar turn); the corner note says the time is not to
