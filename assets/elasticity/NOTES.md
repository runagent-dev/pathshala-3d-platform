# Notes: Elasticity (স্থিতিস্থাপকতা)

Book: physics-9-10 › 5.5 › p147–148 (screenshots in `reference/page-147.png` and `reference/page-148.png`; the
owner's reference pictures `reference/ref-hooke-springs.webp` and `reference/ref-stress-strain-curve.png`).

## What the page shows
- p147: a spring or a rubber band stretched and let go tries to return to its length. Applying a force and the
  length changing is called বিকৃতি ঘটা; a force makes a বিকৃতি inside a body, and because of it a counter force called
  প্রত্যয়নী বল appears; take the force away and the বিকৃতি ends and the body returns: স্থিতিস্থাপকতা. There is a
  limit to the force: past it the material cannot return and a স্থায়ী বিকৃতি can remain: the স্থিতিস্থাপক সীমা. A rod
  bent a little springs straight; bent a lot, it stays bent.
- p148: বিকৃতি = (L − L₀)/L₀, no unit ("এটি একটি সংখ্যা মাত্র"); পীড়ন = F/A on a body of প্রস্থচ্ছেদ A, like pressure,
  unit Pa (প্যাসকেল). চিত্র ৫.১১: a rod of length L₀, a weight W hung on it, length L, tension T in it. Box: হুকের
  সূত্র, within the elastic limit পীড়ন ∝ বিকৃতি, so পীড়ন = ধ্রুবক × বিকৃতি; the constant is স্থিতিস্থাপক গুণাঙ্ক. A wire
  of cross-section A and length L₀ with a weight W: T/A = Y (L − L₀)/L₀, Y = ইয়াংস মডুলাস (Young's Modulus), unit
  Nm⁻²; table 5.02 of Young's moduli (rubber 0.01–0.1 GPa … diamond 1220 GPa).

## The one idea to teach
Hang twice the weight and the spring stretches twice as far (পীড়ন ∝ বিকৃতি) and comes back when the weights come off,
but only up to the elastic limit: past it the graph bends and a permanent stretch stays.

## How the figure works
- A workshop (red brick wall, deep blue pegboard with tools, an oak bench), a lab stand with a polished steel
  spring (L₀ = 20 cm), a metre rule whose 0 is the spring's top hook (so the pointer reads L), a hanger and six brass
  slotted weights of 1 N each. A dark line marks L₀ and a red arrow the stretch x = L − L₀ (the reference picture's
  "0, x, 2x"); a bold orange arrow is T in the spring, a purple one W under the weights, the same length.
- The big action leads the story: (১) hang a weight, (২) another (x then 2x), (৩) take them all off (back to L₀),
  (৪) more weights up to and past the limit, (৫) all off (a permanent set), then a new spring. A stepper (W − n N +)
  hangs or removes one at a time; the quiet ⟲ takes a new spring.
- The পীড়ন–বিকৃতি graph on the stage gets a point per load and a line for the order they happened. It shows more as
  the student finds it: Hooke's line (after two weights), the elastic limit (dashed line and ring), hatched "সীমার
  ভেতরে" and dotted "সীমার বাইরে" bands (named in words, and points past the limit are squares, not circles), and the
  permanent set on the strain axis.
- The numbers: 1 N each; within the limit 2 cm per newton (strain 0.1 per N); A = 10 mm² = 0.00001 m², so each
  newton is 1 লাখ Pa and stress ÷ strain = 10 লাখ Pa. Limit 4 N. Five weights stretch 13 cm (not 10), six 19 cm; off
  after five it keeps 3 cm, after six 7 cm; loaded again it stretches along a straight line from that set.
- Test: strain from the rule, stress from the counted weights, x for n weights (Hooke), the elastic limit from the
  graph, why the spring stayed long. Each wrong choice is a real mistake with its own note.

## Open questions
- The book's চিত্র ৫.১১ is a rod or wire; a steel rod's stretch is far too small to see, so the figure uses a spring
  (which the page opens with). পীড়ন is F/A on the spring wire's cross-section, the force per unit area the wire
  carries; in a coil the wire is really twisted, so the constant ratio here is the spring's, not Young's modulus of
  steel. The rule says Y is the name for a stretched wire or rod. Check that this is acceptable to the book team.
- The numbers are made simple (1 N weights, a limit at 4 N, a soft spring); the hanger's own weight is left out.
- The reference curve's points A–E (proportional limit, yield points, breaking stress, fracture) are not on p147–148,
  so the figure names only the elastic limit, Hooke's law and the permanent strain; the spring does not break.
- ∝ is drawn as a small picture in the panel (the bundled font has no ∝); the graph says হুকের সূত্র instead.
