- Negative work: "… কোনো বল যদি কোনো বস্তুর উপর নেগেটিভ কাজ করে, তাহলে বুঝতে হবে বস্তুর যেটুকু শক্তি ছিল সেখান থেকে
  খানিকটা শক্তি সরিয়ে নেওয়া হয়েছে। যেটুকু ঋণাত্মক বা নেগেটিভ কাজ করা হয়েছে ঠিক ততটুকু শক্তি সরিয়ে নেওয়া হয় এবং যে বল
  প্রয়োগ করেছে সে এই শক্তিটুকু কোনো না কোনোভাবে পেয়ে যায়। অর্থাৎ কোনো বস্তুর উপর–"
  - **ধনাত্মক কাজ করা → বস্তুটিকে শক্তি দেওয়া**
  - **ঋণাত্মক কাজ করা → বস্তুটি থেকে শক্তি সরিয়ে নেওয়া**
- "তোমরা নিশ্চয়ই বুঝতে পারছ শক্তির কোনো দিক নেই এবং এটি একটি স্কেলার। যেহেতু কাজ করে আমরা শক্তি তৈরি করি কিংবা শক্তি
  খরচ করে কাজ করি তাই দুটোরই একই একক এবং একই মাত্রা।"
- Boxed: **শক্তির একক J (জুল)**; **শক্তির মাত্রা [W] = ML²T⁻²**.

The page writes both "ওপর" (first use) and "উপর" (later); the figure uses "উপর" (only in one test question).

## The one idea to teach

Energy is the capacity to do work: positive work gives an object energy, negative work takes it away, and in between
energy only changes form (here from height to motion), its total staying the same when nothing takes it away.

## How the figure shows it

The owner's reference picture (a cyclist going up a hill, "Energy in", "Potential energy" at the top, "Energy out"
going down) as a real 3D scene, with the book's words instead of the picture's English labels. Since 0.2.0 the
hill is part of a cycle race track (asphalt, red-and-white kerbs, a chequered start line, grandstands and flags),
not a green field: the owner asked for a race track that is not green.


1. **ধনাত্মক কাজ** (step ১, "প্যাডেল করে পাহাড়ে ওঠো"): the rider pedals up at 3 m/s; an orange arrow along the road shows
   the pedal force along the motion; the energy bar grows as the work is done. The rule quotes the book's line and
   says the rider has to give that energy, from the chemical energy in their body ("যে বল প্রয়োগ করছে তাকে ঠিক সেই পরিমাণ
   শক্তি দিতে হয়"; রাসায়নিক শক্তি is on the page).
2. At the top it is at rest, yet it has 3,430 J: stored by its height (বিভব শক্তি), "এই শক্তি দিয়ে সাইকেলটি কাজ করতে পারে".
3. **শক্তির রূপান্তর** (step ২, "চূড়া থেকে গড়িয়ে নামতে দাও"): no pedalling, no friction; the purple part (বিভব শক্তি) turns
   into the amber part (গতিশক্তি), the bar's length stays 3,430 J. On the flat road it rolls on at about 9.9 m/s.
4. **ঋণাত্মক কাজ** (step ৩, "ব্রেক করো"): a crimson arrow against the motion, the brake pads glow, and the energy leaves
   the bicycle as heat in the brakes (hatched part of the bar): "যে বল প্রয়োগ করেছে সে এই শক্তিটুকু কোনো না কোনোভাবে
   পেয়ে যায়". The last rule gives the scalar and the unit J (জুল).

Numbers (the page has none, so these are chosen and realistic): rider and bicycle 70 kg, g = 9.8 m/s², hill 5 m high
→ 70 × 9.8 × 5 = 3,430 J; at the bottom v = √(2gh) ≈ 9.9 m/s (36 km/h); climbing at 3 m/s (½ × 70 × 3² ≈ 320 J of
kinetic energy while climbing); braking at 4 m/s². The bar's values come from mgh and ½mv² (4.3.1 and 4.3.2 later in
the chapter); the figure shows only the joules, not the formulas, because page 102 has neither.

Colours: বিভব শক্তি purple (#6741d9, gravity's colour in the chapter-3 figures), গতিশক্তি amber (as book-notes), heat
hatched grey, pedal force orange (a pull, #e8590c), brake force crimson (a friction, #c2255c). Every colour also has a
word next to it.

Stylised on purpose (said in the rule while it rolls down: "ঘর্ষণ ও বাতাসের বাধা ধরা হয়নি"): no friction or air resistance, so the total on the
way down is exactly the same. The hill is steeper than a real road (about 26° at its steepest) so the whole climb fits
on a phone.

## Terms (as in the book)

শক্তি, কাজ করার সামর্থ্য, ধনাত্মক কাজ, ঋণাত্মক কাজ (নেগেটিভ কাজ), বস্তুটিকে শক্তি দেওয়া, বস্তুটি থেকে শক্তি সরিয়ে নেওয়া,
রূপান্তর, গতিশক্তি, রাসায়নিক শক্তি, তাপশক্তি (the figure says "ব্রেকে তাপ"), স্কেলার, জুল (J), মাত্রা ML²T⁻².
From the next pages: **বিভব শক্তি** (4.3.2, p106, "Potential Energy"). The brief suggested "স্থিতিশক্তি", but this
edition writes বিভব শক্তি, so the figure uses that.

## Open questions for the book team

- বিভব শক্তি is defined only on page 106 (4.3.2), and with a spring, not height; the figure uses the word on page 102
  for the energy stored by height, explained plainly ("উঁচুতে থাকার জন্য জমা"). Is that acceptable here, or should the
  bar say "উচ্চতার জন্য শক্তি" until 4.3.2?
- The numbers (70 kg, 5 m) are the figure's own; the page has no example.
- The rider's work going into the bicycle "from the chemical energy in the body" follows the page's sentence about the
  one applying the force giving the energy; strictly, the road's static friction does the work on the bicycle as a
  whole. Fine for class 9?

## Polish pass (2026-11-01, 0.3.0)

Against `docs/agents/polish-pass.md`:

- **Layout.** The kit's frame (white panel, no tinted gradient). The panel is lower: the bar keeps only its own
  height, the question keeps room for two lines (the longest, q_dir, shortened), slightly tighter answers on phones,
  and on wide frames two lines for the rule and the worked answer. The 3D now gets 55 % of 390×700 (was 50 %), 55 %
  of 800×500 (was 48 %), 72 % of 1280×800 (was 68 %); every phase and every question keeps the same height (no jumps).
- **Text on the 3D view.** The note "আরোহী ও সাইকেলের ভর ৭০ kg · ঘর্ষণ ও বাতাসের বাধা ধরা হয়নি" left the view: the mass is
  in the first rule ("সাইকেলটি (আরোহীসহ ভর ৭০ kg) …"), "ঘর্ষণ ও বাতাসের বাধা ধরা হয়নি" in the rule while it rolls down.
  The force labels are short names ("প্যাডেলের বল", "ব্রেকের বল"); their direction is the arrow's and the rule's. Left
  on the view: those two, "উচ্চতা … m" and, in the test, "বিভব শক্তি … J".
- **Colour and arrows.** The arrows are bold flat colour in a white outline with a thin dark rim, drawn over the
  scenery, so the orange one reads on the clay and the crimson one on the red kerbs and boards; on a small stage they
  get thicker (not longer), so the shaft stays about 6 px wide. Label text is a darker
  shade of its arrow (orange #b8460a 5.4:1, crimson #b0214f 6.6:1 on the chip; #e8590c was 3.6:1). The pale sand that
  filled the lower half on wide frames is now a narrow run-off beside the kerbs; the hill is packed earth with ruts,
  and the ground and asphalt have a grit-and-pebble grain (one 512 px texture).
- **Smooth.** Static parts are merged by material (`src/bake.js`): grandstands, flags (one vertex-coloured mesh),
  far hills and clouds; the bicycle (frame, each wheel with its tread) and the rider (head, trunk, hands, shoes).
  The crowd is one instanced mesh per stand, so stands off screen are skipped, with lighter capsules and heads (a
  few pixels each). Only the parts whose shadow shows cast one; the shadow map is 512 (the frustum was already
  ±2.2 m, the shadow looks the same). Nothing is allocated per frame (the height line is updated in place), the bar
  writes to the page only when a value changes, and turning the view no longer redraws the shadows.
- **Bugs.** None that broke the ride. Fixed: the three words ran into each other in the narrow side panel at
  640×400 (smaller there now), the label colours failed contrast, and a camera turn asked for a full redraw.
