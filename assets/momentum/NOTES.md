বেগে গিয়ে একটি ছোট গাড়িকে আঘাত করেছে। … কোনটি ছোট গাড়িটার বেশি ক্ষতি করতে পারবে? অবশ্যই ট্রাক, কারণ তার ভর অনেক
বেশি। … সেজন্য তার ভরবেগ অনেক বেশি। ভরবেগ সহজভাবে ভর এবং বেগের গুণফলকে বলা হয়।" Then **p⃗ = mv⃗**; "ভর স্কেলার

Screenshot: `reference/page-71.png`.

## The one idea to teach
Momentum is mass × velocity (p⃗ = mv⃗), a vector pointing the way the body moves; at the same velocity the heavier
body (the truck) has far more momentum than the bicycle, so it does far more damage when it hits.

## Terms (as in the book)
ভরবেগ (p⃗), ভর (m), বেগ (v⃗), গুণফল (said as "ভরবেগ = ভর × বেগ"), স্কেলার রাশি, ভেক্টর, ট্রাক, সাইকেল (the book says
বাইসাইকেল first, then সাইকেল), ছোট গাড়ি, আঘাত, ক্ষতি, একক kg m/s, মাত্রা [p] = MLT⁻¹. Our own words: আরোহীসহ সাইকেল
(the bicycle with its rider, 80 kg), দৌড়বিদ (the runner), গাড়ি (a moving car in the test), উল্টো দিকে, দুমড়ে / টোল
খেয়ে (crumpled / dented), সরেছে প্রায় … / প্রায় সরেনি (how far a car was pushed), ব্রেক কষে, আঘাতের মুহূর্তে, ধনাত্মক /
ঋণাত্মক, মান (size of a vector). Shared with `conservation-of-momentum`: বাম দিকে, "ডান দিক ধনাত্মক (+) ধরে", the
locked hint "পরীক্ষার সময় … বদলানো যায় না", green text #237a35 (arrows #2f9e44), powers as <sup>.

## How it works
- **The book's thought experiment, run by the student.** A town street of three lanes (kerbs painted black and white,
  tiled footpaths, a whitewashed wall). In the far lane a loaded Bangladeshi truck (mustard cab, painted crown over
  the cab, wooden body, khaki tarp), in the near lane a black roadster bicycle with its rider; each heads for a red
  small car parked 5 m ahead in its lane. The middle lane stays empty: the bicycle's labels live there, so nothing of
  one lane sits over the other.
- **The camera stands still until the impact**, framed from the vehicles to the parked cars (at least 14 m of road;
  on a phone the truck's tail runs off the left edge): the student sees both drive up to the cars. After the impact
  it moves along just far enough to keep the shoved car in view. The bicycle is about 42 px long at 390×700, 21 px
  at 390×520 (the tightest). The front view is seen from 33° above with a long lens (8°, about 100 m back).
- **Arrows, one colour per quantity** (as in chapter 3): velocity v⃗ blue #1c6fd9 on top, momentum p⃗ green
  #2f9e44 under it, both from the middle of the body. v⃗'s label sits behind its tail, p⃗'s ahead of its head, each
  at its own arrow's height (so they never stack, and never sit on a body or a parked car). The two v⃗ arrows have
  the same length (0.32 m per m/s); the front view uses a long lens (8°) and lengthens the far lane's arrows by its
  depth (about 3%), so equal vectors are equally long on the screen. **Both p⃗ arrows are on one scale** (1 m per
  200 kg m/s): the bicycle's is 2 m at 5 m/s, the truck's would be 250 m, so it runs off the screen; its one-line
  label sits over the arrow at the edge ("p⃗ = ৫০,০০০ kg m/s →"); the panel line says the arrow doesn't fit (a wide
  view adds "তীরটি সাইকেলের তীরের ১২৫ গুণ লম্বা" to the label). Nothing is stretched or broken: the picture is honest.
  In a small view (under 17 px per metre, e.g. 390×520) the labels carry just the symbols v⃗ and p⃗; the numbers are
  in the table next to it. A pixel check (labels hidden, the scene under each label) found no label over a vehicle,
  a parked car or the cyclist in any phase, direction or speed at the six sizes.
- **Mass tags** ("ট্রাক · ১০,০০০ kg", "আরোহীসহ সাইকেল · ৮০ kg") under the bodies, or beside them when there is no
  room; in the run they give way when nothing fits (the panel has the masses), in the test they always show.
- **The panel**: "ভরবেগ = ভর × বেগ" with the book's formula p⃗ = mv⃗, and the velocity stepper (1–8 m/s, whole
  numbers; ৫ m/s = 18 km/h to start); a small table "ভরবেগ p⃗ (kg m/s) = ভর m (kg) × বেগ v⃗ (m/s)" for ট্রাক and
  আরোহীসহ সাইকেল (Bangladeshi commas from 1,000 up: ৫০,০০০; the numbers stay under 1,00,000); from the impact on its
  corner says "আঘাতের মুহূর্তে" (the vehicles have stopped by then); one line that says what it means now; the actions
  উল্টো দিকে and চালাও; and the book's box (ভরবেগের একক kg m/s · মাত্রা [p] = MLT<sup>−1</sup>).
- **The run, in real time** (a tap on the truck or bicycle, চালাও, Space or Enter): both go at v⃗ over the 5 m gap
  (1 s at 5 m/s), then the crash; the arrows fade at the impact (gone before the end, even at 1 m/s). Afterwards a
  dimension line in the middle lane carries "প্রায় ২ m" (the truck's car), and the bicycle's car says beside itself
  "সরেছে প্রায় ১ cm" ("প্রায় সরেনি (১ cm-এর কম)" below half a centimetre). The truck's brake lights come on at the
  impact and stay on; the line says "ট্রাকটি ব্রেক কষে থেমেছে". The car's back is "দুমড়ে" (crumpled: the back pushed
  in, panels folded, the hatch buckled, the rear glass cracked, the rear bumper hanging) when the dent is over 10 cm
  (from 3 m/s), "টোল খেয়ে" (dented) below. The bicycle bounces back a couple of centimetres and the rider is thrown
  forward a little.
- **Direction** (উল্টো দিকে, ← →): the whole run is mirrored; v and p become negative ("−৫", "−৫০,০০০"), the arrows
  point left, the panel line quotes the book: "ভর স্কেলার রাশি কিন্তু বেগ ভেক্টর, তাই ভরবেগ ভেক্টর: বেগ বাম দিকে (−)
  হলে ভরবেগও বাম দিকে (−)।" (to the right is positive, as in `conservation-of-momentum`).
- **The crash model** (`src/physics.js`, believable, not taught: conservation is 3.5.1): during the impact the car's
  end crumples by `crush` while the vehicle keeps its speed; then the two separate as in a collision with
  restitution 0.15; the parked car skids on locked tyres (0.7 g), the truck brakes at 5.5 m/s², the rider at
  3 m/s². The crumple depth comes from the energy lost (½ μ (1 − e²) u², a share of it into a 400 kN/m crumple
  zone): 22 cm from the truck at 5 m/s, 5 cm from the bicycle. Pushed: truck 0.08 m at 1 m/s, 2.0 m at 5, 5.1 m at
  8; bicycle 0.05–3.4 cm. The unit tests check the car always stays ahead of the vehicle at every speed.
- Skid marks or an outline of where the car stood were tried in thought but left out: in the side view the truck
  stands on that very spot after the crash, so they would be hidden; the dimension line in the empty middle lane
  shows the shove instead.
- **Test** (5 questions, one of each kind, new order each time, each on its own picture with its own numbers, so no
  picture or answer gives away another; every mass and velocity a question needs is on the picture; the momentum
  arrows hidden until the answer):
  1. which does more damage and why (truck and bicycle at 3 or 6 m/s): the truck, far more momentum / the same,
     same velocity / the truck, heavier though their momenta are equal / the truck, only it has momentum; the answer
     replays the crash with the momentum arrows;
  2. p of one body read off the picture (truck 2 m/s, car 5, runner 5, bicycle 7): m v / m ÷ v / m + v / m v²
     (four different numbers);
  3. two bodies, which has more momentum (a car at 1 m/s vs a runner at 4; a runner at 4.5 vs a bicycle at 3; a car
     at 0.4 vs a bicycle at 5, equal): either body / both the same / can't be compared;
  4. the bicycle's velocity doubled (its p shown): 2p / 4p (as if with v²) / p / p ÷ 2; the answer speeds it up;
  5. a body moving left with only the size of its velocity shown ("v⃗ এর মান ৪ m/s"), right positive: −m v / +m v /
     −m ÷ v / +m ÷ v.
  Every wrong choice gets its own line on that mistake. During the test the scene keys and taps rest, with the
  locked hint at the top of the view.
