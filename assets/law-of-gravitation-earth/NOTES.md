# Notes: The Earth's pull and weight (পৃথিবীর টান ও ওজন)

Book: physics-9-10 › 3.7 › p79 (`order: 2`, after `law-of-gravitation`, the two spheres). Screenshot:
`reference/page-78.png`.

## What the page shows
The second half of 3.7: the Earth pulls a body on its surface with F = GMm/R², R measured from the Earth's centre
(the Earth's mass acts as if it were all at its centre); that pull is the body's weight; for each kg it is the same,
g = GM/R² ≈ 9.8 m s⁻², with the book's M = 5.98 × 10²⁴ kg and R = 6.37 × 10⁶ m.

## The one idea to teach
A body's weight is the Earth's gravitational pull on it, measured from the Earth's centre: about 9.8 N for each kg,
the same for every kg (g), and the body pulls the Earth just as hard.

## What the student does
One scene: the Earth cut open like an orange to its centre (inner core, outer core, mantle, crust; Dhaka at the top)
in a starry sky, an iron 1 kg bazaar weight drawn very much bigger than life. One big button: "১ বাটখারাটি
পৃথিবীতে রাখো" (two equal outlined violet arrows, ওজন and পৃথিবীর উপর টান; the R line grows from the centre),
"২ আরেকটি বাটখারা উপরে রাখো" (twice the weight; the rule: the same pull for each kg, then g = G × M ÷ R²), then
"আবার প্রথম থেকে দেখো". Test: 3 questions (does the weight pull the Earth; R for a weight 1 m up; the weight two
radii from the centre).

## Terms (as in the book)
মহাকর্ষ বল, মহাকর্ষীয় ধ্রুবক G, ভর, দূরত্ব, আকর্ষণ করে, নিজের দিকে, পৃথিবীর কেন্দ্র, কেন্দ্রবিন্দু, পৃথিবীপৃষ্ঠ, ব্যাসার্ধ, ওজন, মাধ্যাকর্ষণ (বল), ত্বরণ, g. The book's যেকোনো, কেন্দ্রের দিকে. The unit vector r̂ (একক ভেক্টর) is "for higher classes": the figure only shows the direction of the pull with the arrows and never writes r̂. Our own words: গোলক (the iron ball), বাম গোলক / ডান গোলক (in sentences, instead of m₁ / m₂), বাটখারা (the 1 kg bazaar weight), তীর, টান (the pull, in plain sentences; মহাকর্ষ বল is its name, said once on the first screen), ২ গুণ, ৪ ভাগের ১ ভাগ, আগে / আগের (the faint arrow, and what the pull is compared with), নিউটন (the book writes the unit N; Bangla নিউটন with English N in English), km and m as the book writes them.
