# Notes: Temperature scales (তাপমাত্রার স্কেল)

Book: physics-9-10 › 6.2 › p163–164. Page images: `reference/page-163.png`, `reference/page-164.png` (dev only).

## What the pages show
Topic ৬.২ পদার্থের তাপমাত্রিক ধর্ম (Thermometric Properties of Matter). Page 163 is text only: the property of a
substance used to measure temperature is its **তাপমাত্রিক ধর্ম**; in a mercury thermometer **পারদ** is the
তাপমাত্রিক পদার্থ and the expansion of its volume is the তাপমাত্রিক ধর্ম (alcohol: the liquid's expansion; gas
thermometer: the pressure at a fixed volume; the resistance of a metal; the emf of a thermocouple, −200 °C to 1000 °C).
Then the Celsius scale (ice melts at 0 °C, water boils at 100 °C at 1 atm; Celsius first had them the other way round),
and the Kelvin scale: add 273.15 to Celsius; "তাপমাত্রা 10 °C বেড়েছে বলা যে কথা, তাপমাত্রা 10 K বেড়েছে বলা সেই একই কথা";
a room at 30 °C is (30 + 273.15 =) 303.15 K. Page 164 has the one picture, **চিত্র ৬.০২: সেলসিয়াস কেলভিন এবং ফারেনহাইট
তাপমাত্রার স্কেল**: three glass thermometers side by side (a blue grid on the glass, a dark column), lines across at
100 °C / 373.15 K / 212 °F (top), T_C / T_K / T_F (middle, "now") and 0 °C / 273.15 K / 32 °F (bottom). Text: the
lowest possible temperature, পরম শূন্য (Absolute Zero), −273.15 °C = 0 K; every scale needs two fixed points; for Kelvin
the absolute zero and the পানির ত্রৈধ বিন্দু (Triple Point) at 0.0060373 atm, 0.01 °C = 273.16 K; the Fahrenheit scale
(32 °F and 212 °F); in daily life 273 K is taken for 0 °C.

## The one idea to teach
One temperature, three scales: the same mercury height reads 30 °C, 303.15 K and 86 °F; K = °C + 273.15 (so a change of
1 °C is a change of 1 K); the fixed points 0/100 °C and absolute zero; nothing is colder than 0 K.

## Design
- The book's picture brought to life: three thermometers (Celsius, Kelvin, Fahrenheit, left to right as in the book) on a
  pale blue-grey board on a walnut bench in front of a dusky teal wall. Ivory plates with each scale's own graduations
  (every 2 or 10 °C, every 2 or 10 K, every 4 or 20 °F), a glass tube and a dark mercury column that rises to the same
  height in all three. Horizontal lines across at the fixed points (as in the book), a crimson line at the temperature now.
  One tiny label "পারদ" on the first bulb.
- Labels on the 3D: the name of each scale on top; the temperature now in a solid chip in the scale's colour (Celsius blue,
  Kelvin green, Fahrenheit purple); the fixed points in white outlined chips (hidden where the temperature now already says
  it). Only values and units.
- Panel (শেখো): a row of the book's own temperatures in the book's order (গলন্ত বরফ, ফুটন্ত পানি, ঘরের তাপ, পরম শূন্য,
  ত্রৈধ বিন্দু), one big numbered button that goes to the next of them (the badge rings the top of the Celsius column), the
  three readings, T_K = T_C + 273.15 with the student's numbers, a line that says "১০ °C বেড়েছে বলা আর ১০ K বেড়েছে বলা একই কথা"
  once the student has dragged the mercury away from where the step put it, and the rule in the book's words (which
  changes with the step). A drag on the mercury (or the arrow keys) sets any temperature in whole degrees, never below
  absolute zero.
- Absolute zero: the thermometers' vertical scale changes (−12 … 108 °C becomes −300 … 108 °C), so the mercury falls to
  the bottom of the tubes and the 0 °C and 100 °C lines move up; the tubes are an idealised picture (no real mercury
  thermometer goes that low). Back to the other steps, the scale returns.
- The triple point (0.01 °C = 273.16 K, 32.02 °F) is shown as numbers and the rule's words; the book gives no picture of
  the sealed cell, so none is drawn.
- Test: 5 questions, shuffled and with new numbers each time (Celsius → Kelvin, Kelvin → Celsius, a rise of x °C is a rise of
  x K, absolute zero in °C, boiling or melting in °F); the picture shows only the scale the question names until the answer;
  every wrong choice a real mistake with its own note.

## Terms (as in the book)
তাপমাত্রা, তাপমাত্রিক ধর্ম, তাপমাত্রিক পদার্থ, পারদ, সেলসিয়াস (°C), কেলভিন (K), ফারেনহাইট (°F), পরম শূন্য (Absolute Zero),
পানির ত্রৈধ বিন্দু (Triple Point), স্কেল, বরফ গলে, পানি ফুটতে থাকে / বাষ্পীভবন, ঘরের তাপমাত্রা. Units as Latin symbols °C, K, °F, atm.

## Open questions
- The book's figure shows only 0 °C … 100 °C; the figure adds the whole way down to absolute zero (an idealised tube).
- 86 °F for 30 °C and the other Fahrenheit values come from T_F = 9/5 T_C + 32 (the book gives only 32 °F and 212 °F).
- The first temperature on the screen is 30 °C (the book's room example) before any step is chosen.
- The other thermometric properties (gas pressure, resistance, thermocouple emf) have no picture in the book and are not drawn.
- Every Bangla wording needs a person's check against the book.
