## The one idea to teach
Here, through the classic home experiment (not in the book's text): a coin at rest on a card wants to stay at rest when the card is flicked away (স্থিতি জড়তা), so it falls straight into the glass. The book's idea:
A body wants to keep doing what it is doing: at rest it wants to stay at rest (স্থিতি জড়তা: the bus starts, the feet
go with it, the upper body stays, you lean back); moving it wants to keep moving (গতি জড়তা: step off a moving bus,
the feet stop, the upper body runs on, you tumble forward).

## Terms (as in the book)
জড়তা, স্থিতি জড়তা, গতি জড়তা, স্থির থাকতে চায়, গতিশীল থাকতে চায়, পেছনের দিকে হেলে পড়া, হুমড়ি খেয়ে পড়া,
শরীরের নিচের অংশ, শরীরের ওপরের অংশ. The book writes ওপরের three times in the স্থিতি জড়তা paragraph and উপরের once in
the next; the figure uses ওপরের throughout (the book's own form here, and the term list of this topic). The passenger
is "ছেলেটি / সে" everywhere (the book's "সে"); the book's general sentences keep "আমরা".

## How the figure works
- A card on a glass on a wooden table, a ৫ টাকা coin on the card. A tap on the view (or the action) plays it: a finger
  flicks the card (2.2 m/s), shown 10 times slower (said in the panel). The card shoots off; friction (μ = 0.25) acts
  on the coin only while the card slides under it (≈ 25 ms), so the coin gets only ≈ 7 cm/s and falls straight into
  the glass, bounces once and rests on the bottom.
- A blue arrow on the card and an orange one on the coin show each one's speed along the table; the panel gives both
  speeds in m/s and why (স্থিতি জড়তা); labels say what each does while it is true (হঠাৎ সরে যায় / স্থির থাকতে চায় /
  সোজা নিচে পড়ে).
- ← → step through the event, Space plays or pauses, F/V views. The lab starts from the front; a drag turns it.

## The test
3 questions, shuffled: what happens to the coin if the card is flicked (asked before the flick), which inertia it is
and why (the wrong answers: inertia of motion, the card staying, the finger pushing the coin), which one moves fast
just after the flick (the card; the coin hardly moves). After each answer the scene plays on with its arrows.
