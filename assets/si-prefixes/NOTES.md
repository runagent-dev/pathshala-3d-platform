# Notes: SI prefixes, how big is it?

Feedback and change requests. Newest at the top. (Written from the dashboard's Notes tab; stays out of the handoff zip.)

## Polish pass (2026-11-01), v0.4.0
- Layout: the 3D view on top, one strip under it at every size (never over the 3D): the caption (prefix chip, object, size in Bangla, 10ⁿ m and the note) and the controls (play/pause, then the 17 stops = the rows of Table 1.05, smallest first, each with its prefix, 10ⁿ and object). The old Table 1.05 box and the caption that sat over the picture are gone; the strip does both jobs. The caption keeps room for its tallest text, so the 3D never changes height. On short frames (≤ 460 px tall) caption and controls sit side by side in the strip. 3D share: 77 % at 390×700, 76 % at 844×390, 70 % at 800×500.
- On the 3D view only short labels: the scale bar (on a solid chip) and the pins (বাংলাদেশ, "… এতটুকু", সূর্য, প্রক্সিমা সেন্টরি; "নিকটতম তারার দূরত্ব এতটুকু" shortened to "তারার দূরত্ব এতটুকু"). Pin dots are hollow, so the place they mark (Bangladesh) shows through. While moving, the caption says the journey and what is next, and its chip and the strip light up each prefix as it passes.
- Setting: no more white page behind the objects. Each stop has its own coloured backdrop (one shader quad): violet/indigo for quark and nucleus, deep blue for the atom, slate for DNA, warm plasma for the blood cells, sand for the ant, a day sky with soft clouds from the ball to Padma Bridge, starry night space from the Earth to the galaxy. Colours blend between stops; motes (small world) and stars (space) stream outward while zooming in. Object colours adjusted where they met their backdrop (atom rings and electrons, DNA strands and rungs, quark circle, Earth's orbit, the star line, a glowing additive galaxy).
- Speed: draws only while playing or flying to a chosen stop; paused, held, or finished (loop off) it draws nothing (was ~20 frames/s at all times, objects kept turning). Draw calls avg 178 → 11, max 352 → 52; triangles max 56k → 37k (headless, 390×700). Stitches, DNA rungs, nucleons, electrons, bridge piers and ant legs are instanced; the student, the bus, the goals, banks and roads merged per material; one shared glow texture; Earth and field textures 1024 px (power of two). DOM written only when it changes (no per-frame layout reads).
- Bridge: main.js uses startFigure; new `seek` / `duration` (the video export now renders exact frames) and `reset`. `get` also lists the stops.
- Bugs fixed: starting at a stop (`start=`, `goto` without animation) could show the end of the move before it (a rounding error in the loop's modulo); with loop off it played on (and kept drawing) after the end; a seek during a flight was ignored; space on a focused stop button played or paused instead of choosing that stop; the screen reader heard every prefix flying past (now one line per stop); the bus poked into the student's frame on wide screens (stops spaced a little wider).
