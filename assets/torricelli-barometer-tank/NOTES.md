# Notes: A tank with a hole (ছিদ্রওয়ালা ট্যাংক)

Book: physics-9-10 › 5.4.1 › p146. Screenshot: `reference/page-145.png` (both pages). The owner's picture:
`reference/owner-jet.png` (a tank with water height H, a hole at depth h, the jet's range R: Torricelli's law of
efflux). Split from `torricelli-barometer` (1.0.0), where it was the sixth of six scenes.

## What the page shows
Torricelli's tube: "নলের উপরে কোনো ফুটো নেই, তাই সেদিক দিয়ে বাতাস চাপ দিতে পারছে না", and the air pressing on the
dish holds the column. The tank is the owner's addition: the same idea with water and a lid.

## The one idea to teach
With the lid on nothing presses on the water from above, and the outside air holds the water in at the hole; take
the lid off and the column of water above the hole (hρg) pushes a jet out: the bigger h, the stronger the jet.

## What the student does
One object, one button, one idea (the owner: "too much; should be easier"). ছিপি খোলো (lid on): a few drops, then it
stops; an outlined blue arrow shows the air pressing on the hole. ঢাকনা খোলো: the arrow moves to the water's surface,
a jet spurts out and, as the tank drains (shown faster), h falls and the jet weakens. আবার ভরে ঢাকনা দাও refills it.
The panel: one short line, h, the rule. Test: 3 questions (lid on, lid off, draining).

The book does not teach the efflux speed v = √(2gh), so no formula from it appears; the jet is drawn on its true
parabola (reach 2√(h·fall)) but only "h বেশি, ধারা জোরে" is said.

## Terms (as in the book)
বাতাসের চাপ, পানির স্তম্ভ, hρg (topic 5.3), টরিসেলির নল; ট্যাংক, ঢাকনা, ছিপি, ছিদ্র, ধারা.
