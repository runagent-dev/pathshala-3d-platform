# Notes: Speed and velocity (দ্রুতি এবং বেগ)

Book: physics-9-10 › 2.5 › p40 (the section runs onto p41)

## What the page shows
Section ২.৫ দ্রুতি এবং বেগ (Speed and Velocity), text only (no picture of its own). দ্রুতি is the rate of change of
দূরত্ব with time (20 সেকেন্ডে 100 m → v = 100 m / 20 s = 5 m/s; মাত্রা [v] = LT⁻¹). বেগ is the rate of change of
সরণ with time (50 m in a given direction in 20 s → 2.5 m/s, in that direction); বেগ is a ভেক্টর, so its দিক must be
given. In সরল রৈখিক গতি the two hardly differ: বেগের মানটিই দ্রুতি. Then উদাহরণ on page 41: the winding road of
চিত্র ২.০৪ (page 39), by cycle from A to B in 20 minutes: গড় দ্রুতি = মোট অতিক্রান্ত দূরত্ব / মোট সময় (the
screenshot is cut off there; on চিত্র ২.০৪ A→B is 4 km along the road and the সরণ is 2 km). Screenshot:
`reference/page-40.png` (pages 40 and 41).

## The one idea to teach
দ্রুতি = দূরত্ব ÷ সময়: how fast, no direction. বেগ = সরণ ÷ সময়: with a direction (a vector). Something can keep the
same দ্রুতি while its বেগ keeps changing, because its দিক changes.

## Terms (as in the book)
দ্রুতি, বেগ, দূরত্ব, সরণ, সময়, পরিবর্তনের হার, মাত্রা, গড় দ্রুতি, গড় বেগ, মোট অতিক্রান্ত দূরত্ব, মোট সময়,
ভেক্টর, দিক, মান (বেগের মান), সরল রৈখিক গতি; m, s, m/s, km, মিনিট, km/h. Compass words: উত্তর, দক্ষিণ, পূর্ব,
পশ্চিম, উত্তর-পূর্ব …

## How it works
- Two examples, two chips in the panel: **পাথর** (first) and **সাইকেল**. View: উপর থেকে (from above, north up) or
  ঘুরিয়ে দেখো (orbit).
- **পাথর**: a student whirls a stone on a jute rope over the head (radius 1 m, 4 m/s; the rope hangs at the angle of
  a conical pendulum, tan = g r / v²). Shown 3 times slower (a note says so). The বেগ is an orange arrow along the
  circle; faint copies of it are left every quarter turn and fade within a turn, so the arrow is seen to keep its
  length but not its direction. Panel: দ্রুতি = ৬.২৮ m (এক পাকে দূরত্ব) ÷ ১.৫৭ s (এক পাকে সময়) = ৪ m/s; বেগ = ৪ m/s
  (মান) and the direction (দিক) as a turning arrow and a compass word that changes every 45°. A compass is drawn
  on the grass.
- **দড়ি ছেড়ে দাও** (panel button, or touch the student's hand or the stone; it acts as the finger goes down, so
  timing is fair): the stone flies straight on along the বেগ it had at that moment (the tangent), falling honestly
  (g = 9.8 m/s², it lands about 0.5 s and 2 m on, then slides to a stop or sinks in the pond). The বেগ at the
  moment of letting go stays drawn at that place, and dots mark the way it went. The game: land it in the pond.
  It works only if you let go when the বেগ arrow points at the pond; letting go when the stone is between the
  student and the pond (thinking it flies outwards) misses by a quarter turn, and the hint says why.
- **সাইকেল**: the road of চিত্র ২.০৪ as a small 3D map (traced from the book; km posts ০–৭, A and B). The cyclist rides
  A→B at a steady 12 km/h (20 minutes shown in 8 s), or can be dragged along the road. The road travelled is drawn
  blue (দূরত্ব), the straight arrow from A orange (সরণ). Panel: গড় দ্রুতি = দূরত্ব ÷ সময় and গড় বেগ = সরণ ÷ সময়,
  worked live; at B: ৪ km ÷ ২০ মিনিট = ১২ km/h and ২ km ÷ ২০ মিনিট = ৬ km/h, towards B (২০ মিনিট = ১/৩ ঘণ্টা).
- Colours: দূরত্ব and দ্রুতি blue, সরণ and বেগ orange, সময় purple, in the panel and the 3D.
- Test (৫ questions, tap answers, the 3D sets each one up): which arrow (ক–ঘ) the stone goes along if let go now
  (tangent forwards; wrong: outwards, inwards, backwards), then it is let go; the stone at two places: same দ্রুতি,
  different বেগ (four combinations); দ্রুতি or বেগের মান from book-style numbers with both a path length and a
  displacement (wrong: the other one, divided the wrong way, multiplied); গড় দ্রুতি or গড় বেগ on the road in
  10/20/40 minutes (wrong: the other quantity, 20 minutes taken as 0.2 hour, minutes not turned into hours);
  the average velocity of a whole turn (0; wrong: the speed, the diameter or the radius ÷ the time).
- Keys: space/enter let go / whirl again / ride or pause, 1 and 2 the examples, ← → move the cycle, f/v views.
