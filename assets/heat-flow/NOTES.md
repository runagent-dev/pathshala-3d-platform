# Notes: Internal Energy and Flow of Heat (অভ্যন্তরীণ শক্তি ও তাপের প্রবাহ)

Book: physics-9-10 › 6.1.1 › p162 (`reference/page-162.png`)

## What the page shows
Topic 6.1.1 "অভ্যন্তরীণ শক্তি ও তাপের প্রবাহ (Internal Energy and Flow of Heat)". The only picture is **চিত্র ৬.০১**: a tall
narrow tube on the left with its water surface high, and a wide open vessel on the right with a lower, wavy surface,
joined low down by a pipe with a tap (a tan plug, a brown knob). Caption: "তাপমাত্রা তরলের উচ্চতার মতো, তাপ
তরলের আয়তনের মতো।" The text says: a hot pin holds far less heat energy than a glass of water, yet dipped into it it gives
some of it away, because the flow of heat depends on temperature, not on the amount of heat; heat always goes from the
higher temperature to the lower until the two are equal; temperature is a measure of the average kinetic energy of the
molecules inside a substance; the analogy: join two vessels with a pipe, water goes from the higher surface to the lower
until the heights are equal, whichever holds more water.

## The one idea to teach
Heat goes from the higher temperature to the lower, not from the body with more heat: like water between two vessels, it
runs from the greater height to the lower until the two are equal (the vessel with far more water can still be the lower
one and receive).

## What the figure does
- The book's picture in 3D, same arrangement: tube left, vessel right, pipe and tap low. One tap on the tap or on the
  big numbered button ("১ কলটি খোলো") opens it; water runs (quick at first, then slower: the difference halves about
  every 0.7 s on screen), stops when the surfaces are level. The tube falls from 22 to about 8.8 cm, the vessel rises
  from 8 to 8.8 cm (the area ratio is 16: the vessel holds ~6 times the water of the tube at the start).
- A dashed level line at each surface, one outlined arrow between them while water runs, short labels ("উচ্চতা ২২ সেমি").
- Chip "তাপের প্রবাহ": the same picture as the hot pin (tube) and a glass of water (vessel), water tinted by
  temperature (orange = higher, blue = lower), labels "তাপমাত্রা বেশি/কম/সমান". Button "১ সংস্পর্শে আনো".
- Test: 5 questions on the picture (direction of water, direction of heat for pin and glass, when the flow stops, what
  is equal afterwards, what temperature measures).

## Open questions
- "কল" (tap) is our word for the valve in the picture; the book calls the connection "নল" and draws a valve without naming it.
- The book does not say the tube stands for the pin; we show it as the book's analogy (height is temperature, volume is
  heat) in the "তাপের প্রবাহ" chip.
- Heights in cm are shown only in the water picture; the heat picture says "বেশি / কম / সমান" (no invented temperatures).
